# Authorise AI-managed Confluence Draft publication

## Decision

Jamie Peppard explicitly authorised Operations Automated AI to publish proposed documents into the controlled **Draft** structure in the existing private Confluence spaces without requesting a separate confirmation for each Draft write.

## Purpose

Draft publication exists to put proposed work in the human reading surface early enough for useful review. Requiring a founder approval before that step adds an unnecessary touchpoint and makes Confluence less useful as the review environment.

## Conditions

AI-managed Draft publication must:

- use committed Git content as its source;
- preserve the artefact's proposed or draft status;
- target the existing managed Draft parent in the selected private space;
- retain the source branch, commit, path, hash and returned Confluence page version;
- stop on a managed-page, title, space or parent conflict;
- perform no deletion, move, archive or external publication; and
- treat edits, comments and feedback as challenge evidence rather than approval.

## Retained human authority

Jamie retains explicit authority over:

- methodology meaning;
- promotion or publication to **Live**;
- merge;
- conflict decisions where a Confluence page changed independently;
- new connections, permissions or audiences;
- automatic retrieval of document interactions; and
- external publication.

This standing authority reduces Draft-publication touchpoints. It does not create self-approval or allow Draft content to be represented as current approved guidance.
