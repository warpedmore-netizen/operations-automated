# Prepare the Operate internal workbench

## Decision

Jamie Peppard supplied the Operate internal-workbench operating model and asked Codex to read and act on it.

That direction authorises controlled preparation of the smallest coherent product increment. It does not approve the proposed product model or change approved methodology meaning.

## Authority granted

- retain and analyse the product feedback;
- inspect the approved connected-work and governance baseline;
- create a separate proposal branch;
- implement a unified **My Work** inbox;
- implement durable Cases, Requests and Tasks while retaining the wider initial dictionary;
- surface existing change decisions and Brand Review work in the inbox;
- implement explainable, correctable prioritisation;
- require explicit human confirmation for consequential approval and risk-acceptance states;
- add proportionate tests, documentation, changelog and assurance evidence; and
- commit, push and open a draft pull request for review.

Jamie's 26 July clarification authorises bounded preparation of the shared operational-graph behaviour within the same proposal: optional parent tiers, human-created and AI-suggested links, visible provenance, non-destructive correction and inspectable derived signals. It does not approve the relationship rules or the signals as operational truth.

## Authority not granted

- approve or alter methodology meaning;
- treat an AI classification or priority score as a human decision;
- accept risk or approve a record without Jamie's explicit confirmation;
- connect a new business system;
- automate operational execution;
- create accounts, external access or customer collaboration;
- merge the proposal;
- publish internally or externally;
- spend money; or
- delegate Jamie's retained authority.

## Separate decisions still required

1. Whether the proposed record definitions, relationships and 80:20 factors are fit for a private internal pilot.
2. Whether to release and merge the implementation after review.
3. Whether later automation, scenario testing, external connections or multi-role access should be prepared.
4. Any future publication or customer-use decision.

Continued use, a passing test suite or a technically complete draft does not make any of those decisions.
