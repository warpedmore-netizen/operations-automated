# Prepare the Operate governed action loop

## Decision

After using the proposed Operate inbox, Jamie Peppard directed that every material decision, approval, review or action still owed by him should enter the system as a work item with a working action. He asked Codex to make those actions work as Operate is built and to base the behaviour on the approved Operations Automated methodology.

This authorises controlled preparation of the smallest coherent action loop. It does not approve the resulting transition contract or change approved methodology meaning.

## Authority granted

- retain and analyse the founder feedback;
- compare the current product with the approved output, connected-work and Human-AI Collaboration methods;
- create a separate proposal branch;
- give every open Operations Bible state a working governed action;
- route specialist My Work items into their existing controlled workflow;
- require explicit founder confirmation and retained rationale for consequential actions;
- prevent direct status changes from bypassing the action contract;
- retain action, actor, prior state, resulting state, evidence and outcome in activity history;
- update the product proposal, assurance evidence, priorities and changelog;
- run automated and live interface checks; and
- commit, push and open a draft pull request for review.

## Authority not granted

- approve the proposed action vocabulary or transition rules;
- change approved methodology meaning;
- infer a decision from a click, silence, technical readiness or continued use;
- accept risk, approve a Change or record Jamie's material judgement without his exact action;
- execute external operational work;
- connect another system;
- merge or release the product;
- publish internally or externally;
- spend money; or
- delegate Jamie's retained authority.

## Separate decisions still required

1. Whether the governed action contract is suitable for a bounded private pilot.
2. Whether the action-loop branch may merge into the proposed Operate branch.
3. Whether the complete proposed Operate product may later merge or release.
4. Any future external connection, multi-user authority model, notification, automatic execution or customer-use decision.

Successful tests and working buttons do not make those decisions.
