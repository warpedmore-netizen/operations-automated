# Prepare the Workbench operating surface

## Exact bounded decision

Jamie Peppard directed Codex to finish the Operations Automated Workbench as the actual operating surface and explicitly stated that this is an implementation task, not a request for another architecture proposal or future roadmap.

This records authority to implement, test, document, commit, push and open a draft pull request from the latest Operate Workbench branch.

## Authorised preparation

- governed repository manifest, broader indexing, heading chunks, authority labels, baseline FTS5 retrieval and optional configured embeddings;
- exact knowledge snapshots and citations;
- recent-message, rolling-summary and active-work conversation continuity;
- versioned executable Operations Bible and configurable Work Profiles;
- separate correctable type and Work Profile recommendations;
- unified My Work filters and source-backed specialist queue projection;
- one shared Decision/Approval representation;
- first-class external-Codex Build Jobs and structured implementation receipts;
- exact founder release confirmation before merge authority;
- safe additive SQLite migration and restart recovery;
- clean, existing-database, automated, desktop and phone-width checks;
- user guidance, product documentation, changelog, version impact and assurance evidence; and
- a commit, push and draft pull request.

## Still unauthorised

- approval of the proposed product behaviour;
- methodology meaning change;
- merge or release;
- internal or external publication;
- a direct or autonomous Codex connection;
- a new external connection;
- spending or risk acceptance outside an exact recorded decision;
- customer data or customer use;
- deployment; and
- delegation of Jamie's retained authority.

## Separate decisions still required

1. Whether the proposed 1.4 operating surface is suitable for bounded private founder use.
2. Whether its draft pull request may merge into the Operate proposal stack.
3. Whether the combined proposed Workbench may later merge or release.
4. Any Confluence publication produced after an authorised methodology merge.
5. Any future hosted, multi-user, notification or direct-Codex connection.

Passing tests and a technically complete draft do not make these decisions.

## Outcome-ticket implementation addendum

Jamie's later founder review extends the authorised preparation within the same product correction. It authorises readable work references, explicit manual Codex prompts, hand-off and return evidence, direct PR review instructions, a bounded post-approval merge prompt and dedicated Improvement, Change and Operations Bible registers.

This addendum does not authorise a recurring automation, autonomous execution, merge, release or publication. A machine-readable queue may support a later worker, but the Workbench must not claim that work has started until a real claim or manual hand-off is retained.

## Later AI-owner worker direction

Jamie subsequently asked for the recurring or trigger-based worker itself and clarified that AI ownership should lead to review, bounded action and a Workbench update. That later direction authorises configuration and testing of the local recurring worker described in [AI-owned work needs a reliable worker](../feedback/2026-07-26-ai-owned-work-needs-a-worker.md). It does not alter the earlier prohibition on false claims of activity: only a retained worker claim or manual hand-off changes an item to active work. Merge, product release, publication and other consequential authority remain separate.
