Two-minute guide

How the Workbench works

Every action should tell you its result before you choose it. The Workbench keeps the useful answer and your decision in plain language; source, status and control detail stays available when you want to inspect it.

1

Start with My Work

Do Next recommends the few items most likely to matter now. Open any card to take its governed next action, see what happens afterwards and inspect the retained evidence. The score organises attention; it does not approve the work.

2

Capture operational work

Select Capture work and describe the need in ordinary language. Oppa Mate recommends a Case, Request, Task or another Operations Bible type. You can correct the classification and optionally add a Case, owner, deadline, impact or risk.

3

Choose your knowledge input

Open Oppa Mate to type directly, attach a text/Markdown/CSV/JSON document, or select Record. The live level confirms that the microphone is receiving sound. Voice is transcribed first so you can correct it before any reasoning call.

4

Review or recover captured meaning

For voice, correct the original transcript. If transcription fails, the recording remains temporarily in that browser tab so you can retry without repeating yourself. Closing or refreshing the tab clears it.

5

Choose the kind of help

Choose whether you want a direct answer, short summary, fuller analysis, checklist, template or change brief. The main result is written for you; the internal references and processing details stay in the optional panel beneath it.

6

Check before sending

Before you select Send now, the Workbench shows the expected cost and whether anything would block the request. Sending creates an answer in this conversation. It does not make a decision or change the method.

7

Say what the answer got right or wrong

Open Was this useful? beneath an answer. Helpful and You understood me save a reaction only. The other choices first ask for your correction, disagreement, evidence or suggested change, then save it under Saved feedback. None of them edits the method or creates approval.

8

Choose what happens to saved feedback

In Saved feedback, the description beneath the selector changes before you save. Save this use completes that feedback step. Ordinary corrections remain traceable and leave My Work. A methodology or product change candidate opens its separate change review automatically; it does not approve preparation, release or publication.

9

Ask for one useful challenge

Select Send me a challenge for a focused scenario and one primary question. You can target principles, AI suitability, deliberately manual work or the capability needed to deliver change with development teams.

10

Use evidence and publish readable documentation

Open Connections to synchronise evidence or preview the readable Methodology and Internal page tree. Review the create, update, unchanged and conflict results, open any affected page, and publish only after entering the exact plan-specific confirmation. That publication does not create methodology approval.

11

Use the Workbench from a phone

The private route keeps the local server on this computer and uses Tailscale HTTPS between authorised devices. The Workbench, computer and Tailscale must all remain running. Operational activation does not by itself record governance approval.

12

Review the shared brand visually

Open Brand review to see the logo, colour, typography, backgrounds and applied examples. A revision or rejection automatically enters the founder-feedback queue; Codex can prepare a response for your re-review, while overall brand approval remains a separate explicit decision.

13

Continue from the work, not from memory

Select Discuss with Oppa Mate on a work item. The conversation retains that record, its Case, recent messages, older summary, linked controls and corrections, so a short follow-up can be understood in context.

14

Follow one Codex build

After a Change is explicitly approved for preparation, the Workbench creates one linked Build Job automatically. While Codex owns it, there is nothing for Jamie to fill in. Codex returns the branch, draft PR, commit, files, tests, validation, risks and version impact; only then does the separate release decision return to Jamie.

15

Keep release and publication separate

A returned build becomes Waiting for review. Only Jamie's exact Approve release decision authorises the reviewed commit for external merge. An authorised merge receipt reindexes Git; any Confluence update still requires its own reviewed publication decision.

Budgets

Soft limits warn; hard limits block paid API calls. Local records and retrieval remain usable. Limits can be changed in Settings.

What is retained

Conversations, reviewed transcripts, translations, extracted documents, feedback, proposals, decisions, repository references, implementation receipts, source references and usage are stored locally.

What is never automatic

Classification is not approval. Preparation does not edit main. AI cannot approve its own recommendation, and no change merges without Jamie Peppard’s explicit release action.

Automation is not the default

The methodology must also design work that remains manual, identify where human thinking is required and develop the teams and internal capability needed to operate the result.
