# Agentic AI assurance and value validation assurance pack

## Decision in one sentence

- **Decision Jamie is being asked to make now:** Select one non-confidential case and identify any correction required before facilitated use.
- **Later decision after the case:** Approve for internal validation, revise, defer or reject RP-01 and RP-02 separately.
- **AI recommendation:** Run the bounded case before any methodology, product or automation decision.
- **Assurance position:** Prepared for case validation; usefulness and proportionality remain unproven.

## Trigger and founder intent

Public research and approved v0.7 gap statements show that agentic controls and AI value principles are directionally strong but not yet operable enough for consistent evidence. Jamie authorised the research pack as proposed evidence and a bounded RP-01/RP-02 preparation, while deferring v0.8 approval.

## Current and proposed meaning

| | Current approved position | Proposed supporting position |
|---|---|---|
| Agent readiness | A named goal, identity, least privilege, approvals, monitoring, stop, rollback and incident ownership are required | RP-01 organises these into a lifecycle profile, runtime-event minimum and case test |
| AI value | User-defined value, evidence, trade-offs and readiness govern technology choice | RP-02 joins workflow identity, baseline, full cost, outcome, harm and scale/change/stop decision |
| Authority | Jamie and applicable specialists retain decisions | Unchanged; templates and AI cannot approve use, risk, spending or release |
| Methodology | v0.7 is authoritative | Unchanged; both guides remain proposed C2 material |

## Evidence and challenge

- OA-RESEARCH-001 draws on public authoritative, research and practitioner evidence with recorded limitations.
- The approved readiness, lenses, OPERATE and output contract already contain the underlying meaning.
- The strongest alternative is to use existing guidance informally. It has lower record burden but does not provide a repeatable assurance or unit-economics route.
- A core-method change is rejected before case evidence.
- A Workbench implementation is rejected before manual value is demonstrated.

## Trade-offs and residual risk

The guides may reduce ambiguity and expose cost transfer, but they can become ceremonial, encourage sensitive logging or imply false cost precision. The case must remove fields that do not change a decision and retain uncertainty where evidence is weak. Jamie and applicable operational/specialist owners retain residual-risk authority.

## Exact approval boundary

This proposal prepares material for a case. It does not approve either guide, change v0.7, approve v0.8, authorise an AI/agent deployment, connect a provider, collect confidential information, spend money, accept risk, build a product, merge automatically or publish externally.
