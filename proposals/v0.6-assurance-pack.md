# v0.6 combined methodology assurance pack

## Decision

Jamie Peppard approved the combined v0.6 methodology for internal validation on 2026-07-23 and authorised PR #10 to merge into PR #9, followed by PR #9 merging into `main`.

This approval does not authorise external publication, production deployment, commercial activity, new external connections or autonomous consequential decisions.

## What becomes approved for internal validation

- [Actionable and self-guided decision aids](../methodology/actionable-decision-aids.md), including simplicity and progressive disclosure.
- [Human-AI Collaboration Method](../methodology/human-ai-collaboration.md).
- [Activation and first use](../methodology/activation-and-first-use.md).
- [Current methodology synthesis and visual map](../methodology/current-methodology-synthesis.md).
- The updated roadmap, MoSCoW register and traceable feedback records.
- Corrected approval metadata for the evolution system and founder challenge loop.

## Evidence considered

- Fourteen retained founder-feedback records.
- The first facilitated v0.5 case and comprehensive refund workbook.
- Jamie's finding that the workbook was too comprehensive for the initial need.
- A local delivery where construction and tests did not prove that the user could activate or understand first use.
- Repeated interaction corrections showing the need for contextual reconstruction, proportionate representation, mutual challenge and governed learning.
- Live review of open PR state and the complete combined diff.

The evidence is strong for founder intent and internal working direction. It remains weak for independent usability, cross-context transfer and external value.

## Main trade-offs

- The methodology becomes more complete, but could create extra process if proportionality is ignored.
- Adaptive human-AI delivery can improve comprehension, but could overfit to one user or become hidden profiling.
- Activation testing improves realised value, but may add delivery effort or require environment access that is not authorised.
- Practical aids improve independence, but polished templates and scores can create false confidence.

## Strongest alternative

Keep v0.5 as the baseline and continue testing the three additions only as informal experiments.

This would reduce baseline change, but would leave repeatedly observed delivery failures outside the controlled methodology and make their application less consistent.

## Approval conditions

1. Approval is for internal validation only.
2. Start with the shortest useful output and make deeper tools optional.
3. Run a materially different second facilitated case.
4. Test a simplified decision aid with someone who did not design it.
5. Validate activation and first use on one executable and one workflow-dependent non-executable delivery.
6. Review the Human-AI Collaboration Method after seven materially different interactions or an earlier material failure.
7. Retain human authority, evidence boundaries, applicable obligations and recovery routes.
8. Review v0.6 after the above evidence or any earlier material confusion, harm, contradiction or technology change.

- Claims of regulatory, professional or standards compliance that have not been assessed.
