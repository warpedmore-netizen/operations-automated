# Ideas Space 0.1 assurance pack

## Decision in one sentence

- **Decision Jamie is being asked to make:** Approve the Ideas Space 0.1 structure and manual resurfacing process for private internal use.
- **AI recommendation:** Approve with conditions.
- **Assurance position:** Ready with conditions.

The conditions are to test retrieval during the next relevant planning activity and review whether the records remain useful after at least five materially different ideas or an earlier retrieval failure.

## Trigger and founder intent

- **Trigger:** Jamie asked for ideas to be captured quickly, preserved when not currently prioritised, connected to related work and reassessed when context becomes relevant.
- **Jamie's direction:** Create an Ideas Space with structured records, defined statuses, relationship links, contextual review criteria and resurfacing prompts. Capture the Incident Management Simulation Game first.
- **AI inference:** The smallest useful version is repository-native: Markdown records, an index, a template, planning instructions and a local search aid.
- **Uncertainty:** Whether later volume will justify a database, richer relationship graph, scheduled reviews or notifications is unknown.

## Current and proposed meaning

| | Current approved position | Proposed position |
|---|---|---|
| Plain-English meaning | Governance can capture an idea, but there is no dedicated ideas memory or resurfacing practice. | Ideas have a structured home and are reviewed when related work makes them relevant. |
| User or operational effect | An idea may be retained ad hoc and can be hard to rediscover. | Jamie can capture quickly, find relationships and reconsider ideas without treating them as commitments. |
| Human and AI authority | AI may capture and organise ideas; Jamie retains consequential decisions. | AI may also search, surface and reassess ideas; Jamie still controls prioritisation, scope, build and consequence. |

This adds a proposed product/evolution capability. It does not change approved methodology meaning or make the first idea an approved product.

## Evidence and challenge

- **Supporting evidence:** Jamie's explicit request; existing governance already recognises `Idea` and requires source, boundaries, relationships and review triggers; the approved evolution system already connects and deduplicates signals.
- **Limitations:** No volume, retrieval-quality or long-term maintenance evidence exists yet.
- **Contrary consideration:** More structure can slow capture and become administrative overhead.
- **Strongest alternative:** Keep a single unstructured ideas list and review it manually.
- **Why not recommended:** It is quicker initially but weak at relationships, evidence, status distinction and contextual resurfacing.
- **Consequence of no change:** Ideas remain vulnerable to fragmentation, forgotten context and premature rejection or build decisions.

## Affected system

- **Principles:** Supports learning through failure and human-led authority; does not amend them.
- **Operational lenses:** Learning, information, technology, authority, value and risk are relevant during review.
- **Readiness positions:** No readiness position changes.
- **OPERATE stages:** Supports Observe, Prioritise, Test and Evolve; may surface during all planning and delivery stages.
- **Outputs and templates:** Adds one idea-record template.
- **Delivery products and prompts:** Adds a repository planning instruction and local search aid.
- **Existing users, records or migrations:** No migration. Jamie is the initial user.

## Trade-offs and residual risk

- **What improves:** Retention, context, findability, relationship visibility and timely reconsideration.
- **What may worsen:** Capture takes slightly longer; records may become stale or over-structured; simple keyword search can miss conceptual relationships or produce false positives.
- **Unknown:** Future idea volume, maintenance effort and whether automatic resurfacing adds enough value.
- **Residual-risk authority:** Jamie.

## Exact approval boundary

If approved, Ideas Space 0.1 becomes approved for private internal use as the repository record and manual planning check. AI may capture, link, search, surface and reassess non-confidential ideas within the recorded process.

Approval would not:

- approve the Incident Management Simulation Game for scoping or delivery;
- change the approved product roadmap or methodology;
- authorise a database, external connection, notification or autonomous action;
- authorise spending, customer contact, publication, deployment or risk acceptance; or
- approve future idea status changes without the authority required by their consequences.
