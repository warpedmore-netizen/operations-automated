# v0.5 first-checkpoint methodology assurance pack

## Decision in one sentence

- **Decision Jamie is being asked to make:** Approve, revise, defer or reject v0.5 for continued internal validation, including the indefinite founder challenge loop and the proposed connected-work and proportionate-delivery modules.
- **AI recommendation:** Approve with conditions.
- **Assurance position:** Ready with conditions for internal validation; not ready for external publication or a production product.

## Trigger and founder intent

- **Question, feedback or outcome that triggered review:** Seven completed founder responses reached the first planned review checkpoint for the daily methodology challenge.
- **What Jamie said:** The methodology should improve operations as a whole, remain AI-led and human-controlled, learn through continuing challenge, apply at different scales, connect operational work and risk, and deliver either guided questions or deeper analysis according to what the user needs.
- **What AI inferred:** The seven responses support a coherent internal working model covering value, work segmentation, human capability, assurance, resilience, material harm, risk ownership, connected records, proportionality and two delivery modes.
- **What remains uncertain about the intent:** The exact threshold between a short Ask interaction and a structured project requires real-user testing, as do terminology and record depth outside service operations.

## Current and proposed meaning

| | Current approved position | Proposed position |
|---|---|---|
| Plain-English meaning | v0.4 defines the whole-operations architecture, operational lenses, readiness path, OPERATE, output contract, evolution system and facilitated delivery direction. | v0.5 adds an indefinite evidence-backed founder challenge, mutual counter-testing, connected work and risk guidance, and Ask versus assessment/project delivery using one consistent methodology. |
| User or operational effect | AI should return useful analysis and governed next actions, but detailed work-record relationships and delivery depths are not yet defined. | A user may ask a question or begin a structured project. Projects provide fuller consultancy-style analysis and retained outputs. |
| Human and AI authority | Jamie approves methodology meaning and release. Operational users retain value, risk and consequential authority. | Authority is unchanged. AI may guide, analyse, challenge and recommend; it does not infer approval, accept risk or make a user's operational decision. |

The proposal adds and clarifies methodology meaning. It does not merely add an example.

## Evidence and challenge

- **Evidence supporting change:** Seven linked founder responses; repeated correction of product confusion; practical use of reverse, failure, transfer, authority and boundary tests; alignment with the approved output contract and human-control principles; source-backed comparison with resilience, risk and adaptable good-practice approaches.
- **Source strength and limitations:** Founder intent is strong evidence for the intended methodology. It is not independent evidence of external usefulness, adoption or willingness to pay. Most scenarios were hypothetical and no external practitioner has completed the proposed journey.
- **Contrary evidence or disagreement:** AI retained concerns about shared failure modes in AI-on-AI assurance, silent harm, ceremonial records, expert concentration, record burden and the risk that broad applicability weakens product positioning. Jamie corrected AI literalism and the assumption that Ask mode must always answer.
- **Strongest credible alternative:** Approve only the continuing challenge mechanism while leaving both new methodology modules proposed until facilitated external cases are completed.
- **Consequence of making no change:** v0.4 remains usable, but the first seven responses would remain distributed across feedback records without a coherent delivery or connected-work model. The product could repeat the earlier form-led design problem.

## Affected system

- **Principles:** Human-led automation, user-defined value, learning through failure and TIGIPI are reinforced rather than changed.
- **Operational lenses:** Demand and work types; ownership, decisions and knowledge; risk, controls and resilience; information and evidence; technology and delegation; performance and learning.
- **Readiness positions:** Controlled, Improvable, Automation-ready, AI-ready, Agentic-ready and Evolving.
- **OPERATE stages:** All stages, particularly Observe, Prioritise, Automate, Test and Evolve.
- **Outputs and templates:** Output contract, operational assessment, value matrix, feedback records, founder check-in and assurance pack.
- **Delivery products and prompts:** Daily automation, facilitated Ask responses, assessments/projects, future workspace and commercial validation strategy.
- **Existing users, records or migrations:** Jamie is the only validated user. No external users or production records require migration. The parked application does not implement the proposed modes.

## Trade-offs and residual risk

- **What improves:** One coherent working source; visible relationship between work and risk; clearer product entry modes; less pressure to manufacture answers; traceable founder learning; a practical route from chat to retained project.
- **What may become harder or worse:** The scope is broader; AI must select depth well; users may experience question framing as friction; record relationships may feel service-management-specific; continuous challenge may create change volume or fatigue.
- **What remains unknown:** Independent usefulness, answerability thresholds, assurance independence, silent-failure measures, cross-sector terminology, willingness to pay and the economics of an AI-connected product.
- **Who can accept the residual risk:** Jamie Peppard may accept continued internal-validation uncertainty. Operational, legal, safety, security, financial or external-publication risks remain with the relevant authorised people and are not accepted through this decision.

## Conditions recommended

1. Approval applies only to continued internal validation.
2. Connected work and delivery-mode terminology remains working language subject to case evidence.
3. Daily and ad-hoc challenge continues; seven responses are not the end of evolution.
4. Run at least two non-confidential facilitated cases with materially different scopes.
5. Test Ask-mode answerability and project conversion with users other than Jamie before rebuilding the application.
6. Retain open challenges concerning assurance independence, silent failure, record proportionality and cross-sector transfer.
7. Review v0.5 after the two cases or earlier material confusion, harm, contradiction or technology change.
