# Extend Human-AI Collaboration for AI suitability, manual work and delivery capability

## Problem or learning identified

The approved Human-AI Collaboration Method explains contextual reconstruction, analysis, challenge, delivery, recovery and learning. Founder review identified four areas that require fuller treatment: whole-method AI suitability, deliberately manual work, early collaboration with development teams and lasting internal capability.

The Workbench also needs to make the resulting proposal easy for the founder to read and challenge without losing the underlying trace.

## Relevant approved repository sources

- `methodology/human-ai-collaboration.md` — approved v0.2 working method
- `methodology/output-contract.md` — approved user-facing output and human-control requirements
- `principles/human-led-automation.md` — approved human authority and automation boundary
- `methodology/readiness-path.md` — approved progression through operational, automation, AI and agentic readiness

## Affected files or methodology components

- Human-AI Collaboration Method principles
- Human-AI Collaboration analysis and challenge stages
- Automation-suitability assessment
- Manual work design
- Product, operational and development-team responsibilities
- Capability transfer and outcome validation
- AI-oriented methodology representation

## Current wording

The approved v0.2 method governs human-AI interaction and names human authority, but does not yet define:

- how authoritative human meaning relates to machine-usable structure across the whole methodology;
- how deliberately manual work should be designed and improved;
- when development expertise should shape the operational design; or
- how capability transfer should be treated as an expected delivery outcome.

## Proposed wording

Review [Human-AI Collaboration Method v0.3 proposed amendment](../methodology/human-ai-collaboration-v0.3-proposed.md). It adds four proposed principles and working guidance for human and AI readability, manual work, multidisciplinary delivery, capability building and a broader challenge portfolio.

## Rationale

Operations Automated should not equate technological possibility with operational suitability. A methodology intended for AI-supported delivery also needs explicit structure, exception and authority boundaries, while preserving contextual judgement. Sustainable change requires development feasibility and internal capability to be considered during design, not after it.

## Evidence

- Jamie Peppard's direct review feedback on 2026-07-24
- The approved method's existing requirement to ask one primary, decision-useful challenge
- Earlier activation evidence showing that technical completion is not equivalent to usable delivery
- Existing readiness guidance that separates operational readiness from automation, AI and agentic readiness

## Credible alternatives

1. Keep these concerns implicit within the existing principles and assess them case by case.
2. Create separate methods for AI representation, manual work and capability transfer.
3. Treat them as product guidance only and leave the methodology unchanged.

The first is lighter but difficult to test consistently. The second may create fragmentation. The third would leave cross-delivery responsibilities undefined.

## Risks and unintended consequences

- Machine-usable structure could create false precision or become an unofficial source of authority.
- Manual-work guidance could protect inefficient work without sufficient challenge.
- Mandatory team involvement could create overhead for small, reversible changes.
- Capability transfer could become ceremonial documentation rather than demonstrated ability.
- Additional principles could duplicate existing guidance or make the method harder to remember.

## Validation requirements

- Compare human and AI interpretations of representative method components.
- Test a case where work deliberately remains manual.
- Test early product, operational and development collaboration.
- Define an observable internal-capability outcome.
- Assess overlap with existing approved principles before release.
- Obtain a separate founder release decision after implementation preparation.

## Expected cost and model route

Repository comparison and proposal control are deterministic and local. AI-supported challenge or drafting should use the configured methodology-analysis route, display the cost before sending and retain the resulting evidence.
