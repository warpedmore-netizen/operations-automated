# Methodology architecture and evolution system v0.4 proposal

## Proposed change

Correct the project architecture so that Operations Automated is the complete methodology for understanding, governing and improving operations from basic operational clarity through safe automation, AI readiness and bounded agentic operation.

Define OPERATE as its improvement and implementation cycle rather than the complete methodology. Develop the methodology and its AI-managed, human-controlled evolution system in tandem, and park the current application while the useful outputs and delivery journey are validated.

## Reason and evidence

Private use exposed that the project had narrowed the methodology to the seven OPERATE stages and then built a stage-based interface around that assumption.

The current application can retain information and approval records, but it does not provide in-context AI analysis. The user is therefore asked to supply much of the diagnosis, cause analysis and decision content, while the interface mainly reorganises what was entered. This created confusion about what the user receives, what the method covers and why the application should be used.

The triggering signal is retained in the [methodology scope and delivery-output feedback record](../feedback/2026-07-20-methodology-scope-and-delivery-gap.md). A [provisional self-assessment](../pilots/operations-automated-self-assessment-001.md) applies the proposed architecture to Operations Automated itself without treating it as approved.

Jamie clarified that the intended methodology must:

- Improve operations as a connected whole, not only individual processes
- Apply to individuals, teams and organisations
- Address services, cases, requests, incidents, problems, changes, audits and cross-team relationships
- Cover people, flow, ownership, risk, controls, data, technology, resilience and learning
- Move proportionately from operational basics through improvement and automation
- Establish how an operation becomes AI-ready and agentic-ready and then implements those capabilities safely
- Improve itself through evidence and feedback while Jamie retains approval
- Return useful analysis, recommendations and artefacts throughout delivery

## Affected material

- Project purpose and terminology
- Founder Charter
- OPERATE overview
- Roadmap
- Methodology governance
- Product direction
- Working templates
- Future application and AI requirements

The approved application code is retained unchanged. The separate progressive-interface branch is preserved and is not part of this proposal.

## Proposed architecture

1. Operations Automated is the umbrella methodology.
2. Operational lenses describe the connected facets of an operation.
3. A readiness path covers operational basics, improvement, automation, AI and agents.
4. OPERATE provides the improvement and implementation cycle.
5. An output contract defines the value returned to the user.
6. A methodology evolution system turns feedback into governed releases.
7. A delivery system applies the method through facilitated AI before another application is built.

## Options considered

### Continue improving the current application

This would retain momentum in the interface but would continue building against an incomplete methodology and an unproven output model.

### Complete the methodology before testing any delivery

This would reduce product distraction but delay evidence about whether the methodology is understandable and useful in practice.

### Develop the methodology and evolution system through facilitated delivery

This proposal recommends this option. Jamie and Codex use the method on real, non-confidential cases, record useful outputs and feed problems back through the controlled evolution process. Product design resumes when this produces repeatable value.

## Intended impact

- The project has a clear umbrella methodology rather than treating OPERATE as the entire system.
- AI and agentic readiness become explicit operational capabilities with human control.
- Cross-team, risk, work-type and operating-model considerations become part of the core analysis.
- Users receive findings, uncertainty, options, recommendations and retained outputs rather than only prompts and forms.
- Operations Automated uses its own method and governance to improve itself.
- Application development is guided by validated outputs rather than an assumed workflow.

## Risks and challenges

- The broader scope could become too abstract or attempt to cover every specialist discipline.
- Readiness positions could be misused as a maturity score or sales device.
- AI and agentic terminology will evolve and require continuing review.
- Facilitated delivery may depend heavily on Jamie or Codex until repeatable components are identified.
- Parking an existing prototype may feel like lost progress even though its evidence and useful components are retained.

Controls include proportionality, modular guidance, explicit evidence, named authority, real pilots and retaining the option not to automate.

## Proposed validation

1. Review the provisional Operations Automated self-assessment and validate or correct its inferred value order and findings.
2. Complete this proposal as the first full feedback-to-decision evolution cycle.
3. Run at least two real, non-confidential cases with different scopes or work types.
4. Record where the lenses, readiness path and outputs are useful, missing or excessive.
5. Process that feedback through the proposed evolution system.
6. Decide whether the architecture is ready for continuing internal use and whether a new product definition is justified.

The self-assessment has already identified that architecture alone is not a complete methodology. Detailed guidance remains to be developed from evidence, and the current application and notification connections should remain deferred.

## Recommendation

Approve the architecture as the proposed basis for internal development and piloting, subject to Jamie's decisions on naming, module priorities and the evidence required before rebuilding a product.

Do not treat this proposal as approval to publish externally, connect services, collect user analytics, deploy an application or delegate founder authority.

## Decisions requested from Jamie

1. **Method boundary:** Accept Operations Automated as the complete methodology and OPERATE as its improvement and implementation cycle?
2. **Readiness language:** Use Understandable, Controlled, Improvable, Automation-ready, AI-ready, Agentic-ready and Evolving as working positions for internal validation?
3. **Application direction:** Park the current application and preserve it as learning rather than continue interface development?
4. **Validation route:** Use Jamie, Codex and GitHub as the immediate method-and-evolution MVP, beginning with an assessment of Operations Automated itself?
5. **Authority boundary:** Confirm that AI may collect authorised signals, analyse, draft, check, notify and prepare releases, while Jamie retains approval over meaning, consequential connections, merge and publication?

The detailed priority of specialist modules should be decided from the self-assessment and facilitated cases rather than assumed in this proposal.
