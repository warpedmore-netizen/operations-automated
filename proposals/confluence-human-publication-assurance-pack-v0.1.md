# Confluence human publication model assurance pack

## Decision in one sentence

- **Decision Jamie is being asked to make:** Approve, revise or reject the human-first Confluence publication and document-challenge model as the basis for bounded private pilots.
- **AI recommendation:** Approve with conditions.
- **Assurance position:** Ready for a design decision; not ready for live migration or publication.

## Trigger and founder intent

- **Trigger:** The first governed publication created and reconciled 108 pages but exposed repository construction rather than a human reading journey.
- **What Jamie said:** The methodology should read like a book; Internal should contain policies, frameworks, product and functionality documentation, and user guides; Git can serve the technical side while Confluence serves people. Edits, comments and feedback on documents should become challenges to the methodology.
- **What AI inferred:** Confluence needs a curated reader layer, not merely better folder names. Lifecycle and source traceability should remain available as a secondary controlled-record layer. Authorised document interactions should enter the governed challenge loop as evidence, not automatic changes.
- **What remains uncertain:** Preferred chapter length, the first policy set, future placement of the current lifecycle pages, the independent pilot audience, and the future comment/edit permissions and retention model.

## Current and proposed meaning

| | Current approved position | Proposed position |
|---|---|---|
| Plain-English meaning | Every controlled repository document is published as a readable page under Live, Draft or Archived. | Confluence publishes authored reader journeys and keeps the complete repository mirror as a secondary controlled record. |
| User effect | A reader can find controlled material but must understand project structure and choose their own order. | A reader receives a starting point, intentional sequence, document types, explanations, examples and task guidance. |
| Human and AI authority | Jamie approves publication plans; AI converts controlled Markdown without changing meaning. | Jamie additionally approves publication manifests, policy meaning and curated synthesis; AI may draft, map sources and challenge authorised document interactions but must expose omissions, inference and uncertainty. |

This is a product and publication-model change. It does not change the approved Operations Automated methodology by itself.

## Evidence and challenge

- **Supporting evidence:** Jamie's direct use of the live private spaces; the successful 108-page reconciliation; the visible one-file-to-one-page planner; earlier feedback that the methodology needs book or page-tree structure, that deliverables must be human-usable, and that feedback should drive controlled evolution.
- **Source strength:** Strong evidence of founder usability and current implementation behaviour.
- **Limitations:** One primary reviewer; no independent reader test; no completed curated chapter or policy pilot.
- **Contrary evidence:** Lifecycle-first navigation is valuable for assurance and was explicitly requested and approved. A complete repository mirror also reduces omission risk.
- **Strongest alternative:** Retain the current structure, improve only titles and landing pages, and process document feedback manually when Jamie notices it.
- **Why the alternative is insufficient:** It leaves the reader to reconstruct a coherent methodology or organisational handbook from separate controlled files.
- **Consequence of no change:** Confluence remains a governed mirror that is technically reliable but weak as a methodology publication and internal knowledge base.

## Affected system

- **Principles:** Human-led automation, user-defined value, activation and first use.
- **Operational lenses:** People and roles, information and knowledge, value and outcomes, learning and feedback.
- **Readiness positions:** Documentation quality becomes part of operational and AI readiness rather than an output afterthought.
- **OPERATE stages:** Redesign the delivery model; Test the two reader journeys; Evolve from observed comprehension and task completion.
- **Outputs and templates:** Book chapters, policies, frameworks, guides, procedures, references and records need distinct templates.
- **Delivery products:** Confluence publication planner, Workbench preview, source mapping, diff and conflict handling, challenge intake and digest.
- **Existing records:** The current 108 pages remain managed and recoverable during the pilot.

## Trade-offs and residual risk

- **What improves:** Comprehension, reading order, discoverability, task completion, professional presentation and feedback-to-learning continuity.
- **What may become harder:** Authoring and maintaining curated content requires editorial judgement and dependency tracking.
- **What remains unknown:** How much synthesis can be safely automated, which content deserves a policy, the best page length, and the acceptable comment/edit collection boundary.
- **Residual risk:** A polished summary may omit important nuance or appear more authoritative than its sources.
- **Risk owner:** Jamie Peppard during private internal validation.

## Conditions recommended

1. Treat the reader layer and controlled-record layer as distinct but linked products.
2. Keep Live, Draft and Archived available for governance rather than deleting them.
3. Pilot only one methodology reading path and one internal task path first.
4. Require source maps and exact page diffs for curated content.
5. Do not label a document as policy until its requirements, owner, exceptions and authority are explicitly approved.
6. Test with at least one reader who did not design the structure before expanding the full library.
7. Treat each edit, comment and feedback item as a challenge candidate, not truth or approval.
8. Start the document-challenge pilot with manually identified, non-confidential test content.
9. Require a separate connection decision before automatic retrieval or monitoring of document interactions.
10. Keep all publication external to this repository separately unapproved.

## Exact approval boundary

If Jamie approves, the following becomes approved:

- the human-first, two-layer publication model as the design basis for private internal validation;
- preparation of the three bounded pilots on a separate implementation branch; and
- development of the minimum manifest, preview, source-mapping and manual document-challenge capability required for those pilots.

Approval would not authorise:

- changing approved methodology meaning;
- declaring new policies approved;
- deleting, moving or archiving the current 108 pages;
- publishing either pilot to Confluence without a fresh plan and exact confirmation;
- external publication or customer use;
- a new connection, space, permission or data source; or
- automatic retrieval, retention or monitoring of Confluence edits, comments or feedback;
- automatic AI publication.
