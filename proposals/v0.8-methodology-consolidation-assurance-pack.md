# v0.8 end-to-end methodology assurance pack

## Decision in plain language

The approved internal methodology remains v0.7.

The proposed v0.8 Draft now presents Operations Automated as a detailed end-to-end operating method for a business: from context and intended outcomes through journeys, the connected operating system, target design, prioritisation, implementation, activation, normal operation and continual improvement.

The decision is whether that meaning is coherent and complete enough to become the next approved **internal-validation** baseline after review. GitHub integration and private Confluence Draft publication do not make that decision.

## Why the proposal changed

The first v0.8 consolidation reconciled accumulated feedback into a coherent ten-page reading set. Jamie then identified a material weakness: the pages were approximately half a page and the set did not yet amount to the detailed methodology he expected.

The measured position supported that judgement:

- the managed reading set contained ten pages and approximately 3,946 words;
- eight pages contained fewer than 400 words;
- one journey chapter held most of the practical depth; and
- the remaining pages largely described concepts or navigation.

The phrase **consolidated methodology** therefore overstated the practical completeness of the reader-facing result. The underlying approved repository held a stronger architecture, 45-capability coverage model, cross-functional interfaces, readiness, OPERATE, output, activation and evolution guidance, but those elements had not been synthesised into an independently usable manual.

## Jamie's judgement and the proposed response

Jamie judged that the accumulated discussion now provides enough material to draft a much fuller end-to-end methodology and asked for it to be improved.

The strongest contextual interpretation is:

- create one coherent method that helps a problem solver understand, design, change, run and improve the operational system of a business;
- make the practical inputs, activities, outputs, decisions and failure tests visible;
- provide much greater depth without requiring every user to read every chapter; and
- retain honest limits around specialist authority and external validation.

The response expands the managed reader publication from 10 to 20 chapters and approximately 14,050 words. It also expands the central proposed methodology into a 4,694-word normative spine. The start page remains deliberately short; substantive chapters now contain approximately 600–880 words each.

| Topic | Approved v0.7 position | Proposed v0.8 position |

| Reader form | Approved guide architecture and an initial ten-page Draft | A layered 20-chapter proposed manual with a concise entry route and practical depth |

## Evidence, judgement, inference and assumptions

### Recorded evidence

- v0.7 is the approved internal-validation baseline.
- The approved architecture includes outside-in journeys, operational lenses, capability coverage, cross-functional interfaces, readiness, OPERATE, output, activation and controlled evolution.
- The coverage catalogue records 45 capabilities: eight usable internally, 21 outlined and 16 identified; none are yet validated or publishable externally.
- The guide authoring standard expects usable practice guidance to include purpose, evidence, activities, outputs, roles, controls, measures, recovery and tailoring.
- The first Confluence pilot proved private Draft publication, stable identities, version conflict protection and reconciliation without deletion.

### Jamie's judgement

- The previous reader pages were too light.
- The intended methodology should be substantially more detailed and cover a business end to end.
- Improvement should proceed now.

### AI inference

- The primary gap is synthesis and application depth, not a missing core architecture.
- A layered manual resolves the tension between greater depth and proportionate use.
- The central proposal should state the normative method; reader chapters should carry practical application detail.

### Assumptions

- **End to end for a business** means the complete operational lifecycle and its interfaces, not replacement of specialist professional disciplines.
- Jamie's instruction authorises drafting, checks, a GitHub draft PR and publication to the existing private Confluence Draft route. It does not approve v0.8 or authorise Live promotion or external publication.

## Counter-tests applied

| Test | Finding | Design response |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse | More words can create slower, less usable guidance | Keep a short start and method map; allow users to choose the shortest responsible route |
| Boundary | One method cannot competently replace specialist law, finance, risk, safety, security or professional practice | Define operational interfaces and retain specialist authority explicitly |
| Transfer | Small-team and enterprise use require different evidence and governance | Scale depth with consequence, uncertainty, dependency, reversibility and obligation |
| Stakeholder | Local optimisation can move effort, cost, risk or exclusion elsewhere | Keep journeys, affected stakeholders and cross-boundary effects visible |
| Failure | A reader may understand labels but be unable to act | Give chapters practical questions, activities, outputs and gates |
| Authority | A technically workable design may lack legitimate permission | Separate investigation, design, spend, data, release and risk authority |
| Contrary evidence | No evidence contradicts the need for depth; fullness is not proven validity | Retain internal-validation status and a concrete validation programme |

There is no substantive disagreement with Jamie about the need for depth. The qualification is that length is not the acceptance measure. Decision usefulness, coverage, traceability, proportionality, transfer and evidence are stronger measures.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Benefit | Cost or risk | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lengthen each old page without changing the architecture | Fast | Produces repetition and retains missing business stages | Rejected |
| Write one very long manual | One file appears complete | Makes orientation, mobile use and proportionality worse | Rejected |
| Wait until all 45 practices are fully validated | Avoids overclaiming maturity | Leaves no coherent working method for internal testing | Rejected |
| Treat the method as the authority for every business function | Appears comprehensive | Unsafe overclaim; blurs professional and accountable authority | Rejected |
| Create a layered proposed manual and retain honest practice gaps | Practical depth with controlled claims | Requires reader testing and continued practice development | Recommended |

## Principal risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| The word **full** is interpreted as universal or externally validated | Use **end-to-end proposed Draft**; retain explicit specialist and validation boundaries |
| Greater depth overwhelms users | Provide orientation, proportionate routes and progressive disclosure |
| New synthesis silently changes approved meaning | Keep v0.7 current; source-map every Confluence page to approved or recorded evidence |
| Detailed proposed guidance appears approved | Preserve `status: proposed` in Git and Confluence and require an explicit release decision |
| Business breadth creates shallow specialist advice | Ask operational interface questions and route specialist decisions to their authorities |
| Human review becomes ceremonial | Require evidence, time, competence or advice, real authority and challenge/stop routes |
| AI responsibility is mistaken for accountability | Define contribution responsibility separately from human and organisational accountability |
| The method creates documentation burden | Use the lightest artefact set that preserves the decision and combine outputs where safe |
| Confluence edits are overwritten | Reuse managed keys, compare versions, stop on conflict and delete nothing |
| A publication stops after Confluence accepts a page but before the local receipt is stored | Reconcile without rewriting only after one version increment and when title, retained failed-run commit, combined source hash and normalised visible content match; otherwise retain the conflict |

## Checks required before handover

- Every publication page is proposed and source-mapped to approved, published or recorded material.
- The manifest contains exactly 20 unique, acyclic pages under the managed Methodology Draft parent.
- The central proposal and reader chapters state the v0.7/v0.8 authority boundary.
- Front matter, links and source paths pass repository checks.
- Workbench and governance proof tests pass.
- The publication plan reports only Draft create, update or unchanged actions and no conflict.
- Confluence returns tracked page versions, changes none of the separate lifecycle mirror and deletes nothing.
- An immediate second comparison reports all 20 managed reader pages unchanged.

## Validation still required

Before approval or broader use, test:

- whether an independent reader can create the standard outputs without author support;
- a small-team and an enterprise cross-functional case;
- a non-service and non-technology operating context;
- an urgent failure-and-recovery case;
- a deliberately manual design and a bounded agentic design;
- a case where specialist authority changes or rejects the proposal;
- whether the method reveals displaced effort, cost, risk and exclusion;
- mobile and quick-question usability;
- whether artefacts remain proportionate; and
- whether outcomes, recovery and capability persist after transfer.

The 45-capability coverage catalogue remains an honest development boundary. The end-to-end core can be internally usable while specialist practice depth and external evidence remain incomplete.

## Exact decision required

After reading and testing the Draft, Jamie Peppard should choose one:

1. **Approve v0.8 for internal validation** — make the proposed end-to-end meaning the next controlled baseline while retaining stated gaps.
2. **Revise** — identify the wording, boundary, missing practice or practical behaviour that should change.
3. **Defer** — retain the Draft and specify the case, evidence or specialist input needed.
4. **Reject** — retain the reasoning and keep v0.7 as the approved baseline.

No response, positive tone, technical readiness, Git merge or Confluence Draft publication counts as that decision.
