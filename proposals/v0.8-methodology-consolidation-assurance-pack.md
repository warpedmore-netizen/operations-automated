# v0.8 methodology consolidation assurance pack

## Decision in plain language

The approved internal methodology remains v0.7.

The proposed v0.8 draft keeps that baseline and adds a clearer working agreement for manual work, AI responsibility, human accountability, meaningful review, early delivery collaboration, capability transfer and plain-language informed action.

The decision is whether those additions should become the next approved internal methodology after review and validation. Git integration and Confluence Draft publication do not make that decision.

## Why this proposal exists

Jamie asked for the accumulated discussion, suggestions and feedback to be reconciled into one workable methodology and for the draft to be available in GitHub and Confluence.

The material evidence is:

- the approved v0.7 outside-in architecture, operational coverage, interfaces and reader guide;
- the proposed Human-AI Collaboration v0.3 amendment;
- Jamie's clarification that AI should challenge and deliver strongly but cannot currently hold human or organisational accountability;
- founder-use evidence that main answers and actions must be understandable before the user acts;
- repeated delivery evidence that creation without activation, recovery or capability transfer is incomplete; and
- Workbench and governance-product evidence that technical integration must not be confused with approval.

## Current and proposed meaning

| Topic | Approved v0.7 position | Proposed v0.8 position |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | Begin outside-in with the primary journey, then map the operational value system | Unchanged |
| Operational territory | Use the coverage model and cross-functional interfaces; make depth and gaps visible | Unchanged |
| Proportionality | Use minimum adaptable structure and the shortest responsible route | Unchanged, with plain-language progressive disclosure made explicit |
| Manual work | Automation is not assumed; human involvement is retained where needed | Manual work becomes an explicit positive design choice with an owner, capacity, information, control and improvement route |
| AI role | AI may analyse, challenge, recommend and execute authorised work | Adds explicit responsibility for AI's assigned contribution and correction of failure |
| Accountability | People retain purpose, authority, judgement and accountability | Clarifies that AI cannot currently hold human, organisational, legal or moral accountability for an operational outcome |
| Human review | Human involvement must be meaningful | Defines the minimum conditions: evidence, time, relevant competence or advice, authority and a route to challenge or stop |
| Delivery collaboration | Connected operational interfaces are required | Adds early operational, product, technical and specialist co-design where feasibility and maintainability matter |
| Completion | Activate the output and prove first use | Adds capability transfer: the receiver can operate, challenge, recover and improve |
| Feedback | Receiver-centred, retained and governed | Adds an explicit effect-before-action rule and a Workbench-supported route from feedback to proposal |

## Evidence, judgement and inference

### Recorded evidence

- Jamie approved v0.7 for internal validation on 2026-07-24.
- The v0.3 Human-AI amendment remained proposed.
- Workbench feedback recorded the plain-language and informed-action requirement.
- The repository and local Workbench contained ten open PRs plus a later tested Workbench branch.
- The Confluence connection already had an authorised AI-managed Draft route and a separately controlled Live boundary.

### Jamie's judgement

- AI should challenge, perform the work and produce its best output.
- Human accountability remains necessary for consequential decisions and outcomes.
- The accumulated work should be made coherent and workable now.

### AI inference

- The accountability clarification is important enough to state directly rather than leave implicit across existing principles.
- The v0.3 amendment and later feedback form one coherent v0.4 Human-AI proposal and one v0.8 methodology proposal.
- The safest interpretation of “push live” is to integrate the authorised internal working state and publish the methodology beneath Draft, without silently promoting methodology meaning or deploying an external customer product.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Benefit | Cost or risk | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep v0.7 and close the remaining feedback as already implicit | Smallest textual change | Accountability and delivery expectations remain scattered and easier to misapply | Not recommended |
| Approve every accumulated proposal during technical consolidation | Fastest apparent completion | Confuses merge with approval and bypasses meaningful review | Rejected |
| Treat AI as fully accountable when it performs most of the work | Strong rhetorical symmetry | Obscures legitimate authority and real-world answerability | Rejected |
| Require a human to repeat or perform the analysis before every decision | Maximises visible human activity | Creates ceremonial control and transfers avoidable work back to the user | Rejected |
| Publish only a repository mirror | Complete traceability | Does not provide a usable methodology reading experience | Retained as secondary controlled record only |
| Consolidate one proposed reader-first draft while keeping v0.7 current | Coherent review experience with preserved authority | Requires careful status and source-map controls | Recommended |

## Dependencies

- Git remains the authoritative record.
- Artefact front-matter status remains the authority for approved, proposed, superseded and recorded content.
- The Workbench must run from a clean committed source when publishing.
- Confluence Draft publication uses the existing private Methodology space and managed Draft parent.
- Live promotion, external publication, customer use, spending, risk acceptance and new connections remain separately governed.

## Principal risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Technical integration appears to approve every artefact | Preserve each artefact status and state the v0.7/v0.8 boundary in Git and Confluence |
| Proposed wording silently changes approved source meaning | Publish it as a proposed synthesis with controlled source maps and a separate human decision |
| Human accountability becomes a ceremonial click | Require decision-ready evidence, authority, time and a challenge route |
| “AI responsibility” is mistaken for legal personhood or risk acceptance | Define responsibility as quality and faithful execution of assigned work; prohibit self-created authority |
| Manual work becomes unmanaged residue | Require purpose, owner, capacity, information, control and review |
| Consolidation becomes too broad to validate | Use explicit v0.8 change themes and bounded validation cases |
| Older PR code regresses the Workbench | Preserve unique evidence, record superseded branches without replaying obsolete code and run the integrated test suites |
| Confluence independently changed pages are overwritten | Use managed-page versions, preview, conflict detection and no deletion |

## Exact decision required

After reading and testing the draft, Jamie Peppard should choose one:

1. **Approve v0.8 for internal validation** — promote the proposed v0.8 and Human-AI v0.4 meaning through a separate controlled release.
2. **Revise** — state the wording, boundary or practical behaviour that should change.
