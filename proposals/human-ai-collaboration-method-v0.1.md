# Establish a governed Human-AI Collaboration Method

## Proposed change

Create the [Human-AI Collaboration Method](../methodology/human-ai-collaboration.md) as a cross-cutting component of Operations Automated and begin founder-led internal validation immediately.

The method governs how AI understands user context, analyses evidence, chooses a representation, challenges assumptions, delivers useful outputs, recovers from interaction failure, uses memory and turns feedback into traceable improvement.

This approved change extends the v0.5 baseline for internal validation only and remains subject to its recorded conditions.

## Reason

The approved methodology requires useful analysis, human control and continuing evolution, but it does not yet specify the operating method for the human-AI collaboration that delivers those outcomes.

The triggering [feedback](../feedback/2026-07-23-human-ai-collaboration-method.md) contains two connected failures:

1. An abstract, fixed-format methodology challenge transferred too much analysis to the user and did not create a useful conversation.
2. The AI then proposed waiting for sufficient pilot evidence before writing the method, while the intended approach is to create a useful initial version now and improve it continuously through evidence.

This also extends the earlier finding that a delivery is incomplete until the user can meaningfully use it. An AI response is not useful merely because it is correct or comprehensive; its analysis and representation must enable understanding, challenge, decision or action in context.

## Affected material

- Founder Charter and human responsibility
- Operational lenses for people, knowledge, evidence, technology and learning
- AI readiness and agentic readiness
- OPERATE application to facilitated delivery
- Methodology output contract
- Delivery system and future product behaviour
- Methodology evolution and feedback records
- Automation memory and user-working agreements
- Future facilitation guidance, evaluations and product requirements

Approved artefacts are not edited by this proposal. If approved later, a release proposal should identify the precise amendments and migration effects.

## Challenge

### Assumptions

- A collaboration method can improve output quality without becoming a rigid conversational script.
- Interaction evidence from the founder collaboration can produce useful initial guidance while its generalisability remains uncertain.
- User-specific adaptation can be governed without hidden profiling or excessive data retention.
- Continuous improvement does not require changing authoritative meaning after every conversation.

### Evidence and inference

- **Recorded evidence:** Jamie rejected an abstract challenge as unhelpful and explicitly requested an initial method that improves through every interaction.
- **Related evidence:** Earlier delivery feedback found that collecting or technically producing an output is insufficient when the user cannot obtain value from it.
- **AI inference:** The missing component is broader than communication style; it is an operational method spanning analysis, representation, challenge, memory, failure recovery and evolution.
- **Uncertainty:** Evidence currently comes from one user and a small number of interactions.

### Strongest credible alternative

Keep collaboration behaviour within the existing output contract and informal memory, adding preferences only when problems arise.

This would keep the methodology smaller. It is not recommended because it leaves important behaviour implicit, makes failures difficult to compare and govern, and provides no transferable basis for future facilitated or product delivery.

### Possible unintended effects

- The AI could overfit to one person's preferences and mistake them for universal guidance.
- A working profile could become hidden psychological profiling.
- Frequent changes could create instability, duplication or governance fatigue.
- Novel presentation formats could be favoured over clarity.
- The method could become ceremonial overhead applied to simple tasks.
- Visible reasoning could imply access to private model internals that are not available.

Controls in the proposed method include proportionality, explicit information layers, evidence discipline, no silent personality inference, status visibility, materiality-based change and continuing counter-tests.

### TIGIPI

- Would a new user understand what the AI has inferred, retained and needs from them?
- Can a user correct an interpretation or working preference without technical knowledge?
- Does the method recover when the user follows an apparently reasonable but unintended route?
- What happens when memory, a visual format or an expected tool is unavailable?
- Can a person distinguish a recommendation from a decision they have authorised?

## Human and automation responsibilities

The authorised human defines or corrects purpose, value, context, authority and consequential decisions.

AI applies the proposed method, selects representations, researches and analyses evidence, identifies failures, records authorised feedback and drafts reasoned changes. AI does not approve authoritative meaning or infer approval from conversation.

## Delivery and release impact

During the proposed pilot:

- daily methodology challenges should use the collaboration method rather than a fixed questionnaire;
- material corrections should be retained as feedback with their scope and evidence;
- the method should be revised through linked proposals or explicit draft updates;
- memory should distinguish stable working agreement, current context, decisions, corrections and prohibited information; and
- interaction outcomes should be reviewed after seven meaningful cases and continuously thereafter.

If approved for the methodology baseline, follow-on work should:

- add the component to the methodology architecture;
- update the output contract and delivery system;
- define a correctable user-working-agreement record;
- add evaluation examples and failure tests;
- identify product-interface implications; and
- publish migration notes distinguishing the collaboration method from platform policy or model internals.

The proposed method can be reversed by ceasing pilot use and retaining the evidence and rejection reason. No external publication or product connection is authorised.

## Recommendation

Use the method immediately for founder-led internal validation. Improve it through traceable evidence rather than waiting for theoretical completion.

After the first seven meaningful interactions, prepare a synthesis covering:

- what transferred across contexts;
- what was specific to Jamie;
- which principles changed outcomes;
- which elements created friction or overhead;
- what should be revised, approved, rejected or tested next; and
- whether incorporation into the approved Operations Automated baseline is justified.

This is a review checkpoint, not a termination condition.
