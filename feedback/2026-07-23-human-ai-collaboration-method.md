# Methodology feedback

## Source and boundary

- **Source or reference:** Direct feedback from Jamie Peppard during the daily methodology challenge and its follow-up
- **Date received or observed:** 2026-07-23
- **User or operating context:** Founder review of how AI researches, analyses, challenges, communicates and learns during Operations Automated development
- **Permission to use:** Jamie explicitly instructed that an initial methodology should be created and improved through continuing interaction
- **Confidentiality or data boundary:** Non-confidential Operations Automated project learning; no employer, client or third-party information

## Signal

- **What was reported or observed:** The AI first presented an abstract authority question that transferred too much analytical work to the user. It then treated the correction mainly as a communication preference and proposed waiting for pilot evidence before creating a general method. Jamie clarified that the collaboration method should be created now, used immediately and improved continuously through governed feedback and proposed changes.
- **What the person was trying to achieve:** Establish a practical methodology for how AI understands a user's views, reasons, selects a useful form of delivery, challenges assumptions, retains learning and improves the working relationship.
- **Why the current method or delivery was insufficient:** The approved output contract states what a useful response should contain, but it does not yet define how AI should adapt its analysis and representation to a particular user, detect interaction failure, recover from misunderstanding or evolve its collaboration behaviour.
- **Evidence supplied:** Two direct corrections in one interaction: the fixed, abstract challenge did not help the user think or respond; and delaying the method until after a mature pilot contradicted the intended create-use-learn-improve loop.
- **Evidence limitations and assumptions:** This evidence comes from one founder-user and one developing collaboration. The general method must remain proposed until it is tested across different tasks, formats and users.

## Triage

- **Affected principle, lens, stage, readiness position, output or product:** Founder Charter; people and experience; ownership, decisions and knowledge; information, data and evidence; technology, automation, AI and agents; performance, change and learning; AI readiness; OPERATE; output contract; delivery system; methodology evolution
- **People or users potentially affected:** Jamie and AI during the founder-controlled phase; later facilitators, practitioners and users of AI-enabled Operations Automated delivery
- **Frequency or related signals:** Repeated within the same interaction and consistent with earlier feedback that delivery must return useful analysis rather than make the user perform the method
- **Consequence or urgency:** High for facilitated-method value; weak collaboration can make sound methodology inaccessible, encourage superficial agreement and produce misleading evidence about usefulness
- **Related feedback records:** [Methodology scope and delivery-output gap](2026-07-20-methodology-scope-and-delivery-gap.md); [deliverable activation and first use](2026-07-23-deliverable-activation-and-first-use.md)
- **Recommended next action:** Create and immediately pilot a proposed Human-AI Collaboration Method, then make its revisions traceable to interaction evidence
- **Owner and review date:** Jamie Peppard; first synthesis after seven meaningful interactions using the proposed method, then continuing review

## Disposition

- **State:** Proposed
- **Resulting proposal or decision:** [Propose a Human-AI Collaboration Method](../proposals/human-ai-collaboration-method-v0.1.md)
- **Reasoning:** Collaboration is part of the operational delivery system. It should therefore have explicit purpose, principles, evidence, controls, recovery and evolution rather than depend on hidden model behaviour or informal preference memory.
- **Release or retained location:** Feedback, proposal and proposed method retained in the controlled repository; no approved baseline changed
- **Outcome review trigger:** A material misunderstanding, repeated clarification, delivery-format failure, unhelpful challenge, memory error, authority ambiguity or the first seven meaningful pilot interactions
