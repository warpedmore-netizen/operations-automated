# Give Oppa Mate a simple service-account identity

## Source and boundary

- **Source:** Jamie Peppard's direct request in the current Codex task.
- **Initial founder wording:** "Oppa Mate needs branding too ... something simple."
- **Founder clarification after the first visual:** "Oppa Mate is going to be the main service account user at the company - hence Mate in his name."
- **Information boundary:** Non-confidential Operations Automated product and brand material.
- **Authority at intake:** A bounded visual proposal and internal review surface are authorised. Brand approval, merge, publication and use outside the private pilot remain separate founder decisions.

## Jamie's judgement

Oppa Mate is the main service-account user for Operations Automated. The name is intended to make him feel like a recognisable company mate across services, not to name a standalone product or generic assistant.

## AI interpretation

The first AI interpretation treated Oppa Mate as an endorsed product or assistant sub-brand. Jamie's clarification changes that assessment.

The corrected identity is a service-account profile: a controlled **OM** avatar, the full name **Oppa Mate**, and the explicit role **Operations Automated service account**. The avatar uses the shared brand palette while remaining distinct from the OA corporate mark, so a person can tell the company from the account acting within it.

## Challenge tests

- **Simplicity test:** The proposal adds one service-account profile and one reusable avatar, not a separate identity toolkit.
- **Identity test:** The OM avatar distinguishes the account from the OA corporate mark while the descriptor keeps the company relationship explicit.
- **Authority test:** A friendly company identity must not imply access, approval rights or delegated authority; those remain separately assigned and recorded.
- **Transfer test:** The same pattern could identify another future service account without multiplying corporate logos or confusing account activity with company authority.

## Disposition

**Product correction; revise the bounded visual proposal now and return the changed meaning for founder re-review.**

Apply the service-account identity to Workbench navigation, the account heading, first-use welcome and account activity badges. Replace the prior product-oriented Brand Review item with a new service-account item so its earlier review choice is not carried onto materially changed meaning.

## Remaining uncertainty

- The Workbench retained an item-level **approve for internal use** choice for the earlier product-oriented visual. That evidence remains in the audit trail but does not approve the corrected service-account identity; the corrected item requires fresh review.
- The operational permissions, systems and delegated authority attached to the future account are not defined by this branding change.
- No public use is authorised.
- A distinct character or voice expression may be considered later only if real use shows the simple service-account system is insufficient.
