# Methodology depth and end-to-end business application

## Jamie's feedback

Jamie said that the methodology is currently too light: most reader pages are approximately half a page and do not yet amount to a detailed methodology. He believes the accumulated discussion now provides enough material to propose a substantially fuller, end-to-end methodology for a business and asked for it to be improved.

## Operational substance

An end-to-end business operating method must do more than name principles, lenses and stages. It must help a reader move from purpose and business context through diagnosis, operating-model design, prioritisation, implementation, activation, operation, governance and continual improvement. At each point the reader should be able to tell:

- what question is being answered;
- what evidence and judgement are needed;
- what to do;
- what usable output should result;
- what decision or authority is required;
- how depth should change with consequence and uncertainty; and
- what failure, boundary or recovery condition should stop or redirect the work.

## Methodology feedback

The managed Confluence v0.8 Draft contained ten pages and approximately 3,946 words when this feedback was received. Eight of the ten pages contained fewer than 400 words. One journey chapter carried most of the practical depth; the remaining pages primarily introduced concepts or described a reading route.

The description **consolidated methodology** therefore overstated the practical completeness of the reader-facing draft. The approved v0.7 repository contains a stronger architecture and more operational coverage than the publication exposed, but it remains distributed across many artefacts and several practice areas are only outlined or identified.

## Strongest reasonable interpretation

Jamie is asking for one coherent business operating methodology that a problem solver can apply from end to end. The request is not interpreted as a demand to turn Operations Automated into the specialist rulebook for every professional discipline, nor as a requirement to inflate page length without increasing decision usefulness.

The proposed response is a layered manual:

1. a short orientation and complete method map;
2. detailed core chapters covering the operating system of a business;
3. a practical change-and-delivery route from evidence to sustained use;
4. explicit cross-functional interfaces and specialist authority boundaries; and
5. proportionate routes, tools, outputs and review tests.

## Evidence, judgement, inference and assumptions

### Recorded evidence

- The approved v0.7 architecture already connects outside-in journeys, operational lenses, coverage, readiness, OPERATE, delivery, activation and evolution.
- The operational coverage catalogue records 45 capabilities, but only eight are currently classified as usable internally; 21 are outlined and 16 are identified.
- The guide authoring standard defines a fuller usable-practice shape, including purpose, evidence, activities, outputs, roles, controls, measures, recovery and tailoring.
- The current ten-page publication is materially shorter and less actionable than those expectations.

### Jamie's judgement

- The methodology is too light to serve as the intended method.
- The accumulated work is now sufficient to draft a much more complete end-to-end version.
- Improvement should proceed now.

### AI inference

- The immediate problem is synthesis and application depth, not the absence of a basic methodology architecture.
- The reader publication should expand from a pilot outline into a comprehensive proposed manual while reusing its governed Confluence identities and Draft-only publication path.
- The central v0.8 proposal should become the concise normative spine; the reader chapters should carry the explanation and practical application depth.

### Assumptions

- **End to end for a business** means the core operational lifecycle and its interfaces across a business, not specialist professional instruction that would displace legal, regulatory, safety, financial, security, clinical or other accountable expertise.
- The material remains a proposed v0.8 Draft. Jamie's instruction to improve it authorises drafting, checking, GitHub publication and private Confluence Draft publication, but not approval of the methodology or promotion to Live.

## Counter-tests

### Reverse test: can greater depth make the method worse?

Yes. A single long undifferentiated manual could make small decisions slower and hide the core logic. The revision should use progressive disclosure: a short method map, detailed chapters, and proportionate routes that let the user stop when the evidence is sufficient.

### Boundary test: can one methodology competently govern every business discipline?

No. Operations Automated can define the operational questions, interfaces, decision records and hand-offs. It cannot replace the professional authority, standards or detailed methods of Finance, People, Legal, Risk, Security, Safety, Data, Technology, Audit, Sustainability or other specialist functions.

### Transfer test: will the method work at different scales?

The core questions can transfer from an individual or small team to an enterprise, but the evidence, role separation, assurance and documentation must scale with consequence, uncertainty, dependency and obligation. The draft must make this tailoring explicit.

### Failure test: what would show that the revision is still only explanatory?

An independent reader would understand the labels but remain unable to produce a current-state picture, prioritised decision, target design, implementation route, control set, activated change or review record. Each practical chapter should therefore specify inputs, method, outputs and decision gates.

### Contrary-evidence test

No evidence contradicts the need for greater application depth. The material limitation is that fuller synthesis is not the same as external validation. Several specialist practice areas and real-world cases remain immature.

## Change in assessment

The earlier assessment that the ten-page v0.8 publication was a sufficiently consolidated reading draft has changed. It successfully proved controlled Draft publication and provided a coherent outline, but it is not yet a sufficiently complete methodology for the intended reader use.

There is no substantive disagreement with Jamie about the need for depth. The qualification is that page length is not the completion measure; decision usefulness, coverage, traceability, proportionality and tested transfer are stronger measures.

## Disposition

**Propose material change now.**

Expand the v0.8 proposal and managed reader tree into a detailed end-to-end operating methodology. Preserve v0.7 as the approved internal baseline, preserve specialist authority boundaries and mark unvalidated areas honestly. Publish the committed revision to GitHub as a draft pull request and to the existing private Confluence Draft tree for review.

## Remaining uncertainty and evidence needed

- independent-reader usability;
- application to several materially different business contexts;
- depth and accuracy of specialist interfaces;
- whether the chapter structure remains usable on mobile and for quick questions;
- whether the proposed outputs create real action rather than documentation burden; and
- evidence that implemented changes remain effective after transfer into normal operation.
