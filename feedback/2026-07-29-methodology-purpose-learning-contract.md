# Establish the Methodology Product Purpose, Learning System and Delivery Contract

## Operational substance

Jamie instructed Codex to establish Operations Automated explicitly as an AI-enabled, human-led and continually evolving operational methodology without rebuilding it, replacing v0.7 or approving proposed meaning.

The requested outcome is one coherent controlled system in which:

- Product Purpose, users, problems, outcomes, inputs, capabilities, boundaries and product relationships are explicit;
- approved human-readable meaning is connected to a machine-usable form without silent paraphrase;
- material signals retain source, boundary, evidence, interpretation, relationships and disposition;
- related signals can be synthesized without volume becoming truth;
- the Workbench records the exact Methodology version and knowledge snapshot used;
- change proposals show current and proposed meaning, evidence, alternatives, tests, migration and the exact human decision;
- AI cannot approve its own proposal; and
- a released change can be traced from signal through later use and outcome review.

## Evidence and limitations

### Recorded evidence

- Jamie's instruction provides the complete desired product purpose, learning route, Workbench exchange, boundaries, minimum tests and acceptance criteria.
- The approved v0.7 baseline already contains the Charter, operational lenses, outside-in sequence, coverage, interfaces, readiness, OPERATE, output, decision-aid, activation, collaboration and evolution components.
- Draft PR #28 already proposes Product Purpose, Steering, project-boundary, prompt and recovery controls.
- The Workbench already retains feedback, proposals, separate preparation and release decisions, knowledge snapshots, implementation receipts and reindexing.

### AI interpretation

The strongest contextual meaning is to reconcile and connect existing controlled parts, then close specific traceability and contract gaps. It is not a request to rewrite the v0.7 reader Methodology or approve the v0.8 consolidation.

The smallest coherent response is a layered proposal over draft PR #28: strengthen the proposed Methodology Product Purpose; add a proposed learning-system extension, Workbench contract, component registry and machine schema; and extend existing Workbench mechanics and tests for complete signal, synthesis, release, later-use and outcome trace.

### Limitations and assumptions

- The instruction is founder direction and preparation authority, not independent external validation.
- Automated fixtures can verify mechanics but not user value or general applicability.
- Product Purpose, learning-system meaning, machine representations and Workbench behaviour each remain proposed until separately decided.
- PR #28 is still open, so this change depends on or must later be reconciled with that control layer.

## Counter-test and disposition

- **Reverse test:** retaining the current documents without change preserves stability but leaves the Methodology purpose incomplete, machine definitions absent and release-to-outcome trace only partly enforceable.
- **Boundary test:** implementing the Methodology as a Workbench navigation copy would confuse product and method; the proposal instead keeps user-interface structure independent.
- **Authority test:** all new meaning and behaviour remains proposed; no AI-controlled path can create an approved release.
- **Failure test:** if a later conversation cannot show the released version, the Workbench must expose a retrieval or distribution failure.

**Disposition:** Methodology clarification and controlled change candidate, plus a bounded Workbench product change. Prepare one layered proposal and assurance pack; do not merge, promote to Live or claim external validation.
