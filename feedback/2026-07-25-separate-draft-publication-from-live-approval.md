# Separate Draft publication from Live approval

## Source and boundary

- **Source:** Jamie Peppard during review of the proposed Methodology Lab workflow.
- **Context:** The pilot was technically ready but had not been placed in Confluence for ordinary reading.
- **Permission to use:** Retain and use within Operations Automated methodology and product development.
- **Information boundary:** Controlled, non-confidential Operations Automated material and the existing private Confluence connection.
- **Authority boundary:** AI may publish committed proposed material into the controlled Confluence Draft structure. Jamie retains approval of methodology meaning, merge and promotion to Live.

## Operational insight

Draft is a working review surface, not a release state. Requiring Jamie to approve both Draft publication and later Live promotion duplicates the same touchpoint and delays the point at which Jamie can assess the actual human-readable result.

The intended operating model is:

1. AI prepares and checks a proposed document.
2. AI publishes it beneath the appropriate controlled Draft parent.
3. Jamie reads and comments in the form that is easiest to review.
4. Feedback becomes a governed challenge.
5. Jamie explicitly decides whether the resulting meaning may become Live.

## Methodology and product feedback

Operations Automated had correctly separated proposed from approved meaning, but applied release-level control too early. The control should follow consequence:

- publishing a visibly proposed, traceable and recoverable Draft is preparation for review;
- promoting material to Live changes the usable organisational position and remains a human decision.

## Challenge and retained controls

Removing the Draft confirmation must not allow AI to:

- label proposed content as approved or Live;
- publish uncommitted working-tree material;
- overwrite an independently edited managed page;
- create pages outside the selected private space and Draft parent;
- delete, move or archive pages;
- infer that reading or commenting is approval; or
- retrieve comments automatically without the separate connection decision.

## Disposition

**Clarification with material product change.**

Retain the governance boundary, but move the human approval gate from Draft creation to Live promotion. Update the Methodology Lab implementation and use the standing Draft authority to create the reading copy now.
