# Operationalise agent assurance and AI value through a bounded case

## Jamie's direction

Jamie authorised the research pack to be retained as proposed evidence and authorised preparation of RP-01 and RP-02 for one non-confidential facilitated case. Jamie explicitly deferred v0.8 methodology approval.

## AI interpretation

The approved v0.7 method already establishes value, human authority, readiness, monitoring and recovery. The smallest coherent response is supporting guidance and a case-ready record, not a new core stage, software feature or methodology approval.

## Evidence and limitations

- **Recorded evidence:** OA-RESEARCH-001, approved v0.7 sources, their declared implementation gaps and Jamie's current instruction.
- **AI inference:** RP-01 and RP-02 can share one case if it is an AI-enabled workflow with a clear decision and, where applicable, bounded action.
- **Assumption:** a suitable non-confidential case and enough baseline/runtime evidence can be identified.
- **Limitation:** prepared guidance cannot demonstrate usefulness, proportionality, independent review or value attribution before the case.

## Counter-tests

- A new core method is rejected because the research strengthens existing meaning.
- A Workbench register is rejected because manual evidence must prove the decision first.
- A single universal score is rejected because readiness, cost and value vary by work type.
- A low-evidence case must return uncertainty rather than false precision.

OA-IDEA-001 is more relevant because current research strengthens the need for joint resilience practice. Its next action remains a facilitated low-technology exercise after or alongside suitable case evidence; no simulation-platform scope or build is authorised.

## Disposition

Prepare two proposed C2 guides, one draft facilitated case record and a plain-English assurance pack on a separate branch. Retain v0.7 as authoritative. Request a separate decision only after case evidence exists.
