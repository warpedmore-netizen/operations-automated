# Organise the Confluence reading library by lifecycle first

## Source and boundary

- **Source:** Jamie Peppard's correction immediately after approving and merging the first governed Confluence-publication capability.
- **Direction:** Create the readable documents, but organise the library first as Live, Archived and Draft, followed by the normal folder structure.
- **Information boundary:** Non-confidential Operations Automated material already held in the controlled repository.
- **Authority boundary:** The structure may be prepared and tested. The exact live page plan still requires founder review and the separate publication confirmation already adopted for the Workbench.

## Operational insight

The first proposal made subject matter the primary navigation. That is readable, but it makes current and working material sit beside each other. Status labels on individual pages are not enough when a reader needs to know whether a document is current before choosing it.

Lifecycle should therefore be the first decision:

1. **Live** for approved, published and recorded material active for its stated scope;
2. **Draft** for ideas, drafts, proposals and unrecognised working states; and
3. **Archived** for superseded and rejected material retained for history.

The normal Methodology or Internal subject structure should sit beneath each lifecycle folder.

## Interpretation and challenge

The repository status remains authoritative. A document does not become Live merely because it is merged or visible in Confluence.

The order Jamie stated was Live, Archived and Draft. For ordinary reading and change work, the proposed displayed order is Live, Draft and Archived: current material first, work in progress second and history last. All three requested lifecycle folders remain present. This ordering is an AI recommendation rather than a change to the lifecycle meaning.

Recorded decisions belong in Live because they are active project memory, but the page must still display the exact decision scope. A recorded decision is not automatically approved methodology.

An unknown, compound or missing status defaults to Draft. This avoids accidentally presenting uncertain material as current.

## Disposition

**Propose a product revision now.**

Revise the Confluence planner, Workbench preview, product specification and tests before any live documents are created. Preserve the existing human confirmation, conflict checks, version receipts, Git authority and no-delete boundary.
