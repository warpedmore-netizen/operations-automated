# Methodology scope and delivery-output gap

## Source and boundary

- **Source:** Jamie Peppard's direction and repeated feedback during private application testing and project review.
- **Date received or observed:** 2026-07-20.
- **User or operating context:** Founder, methodology owner and first private-test user.
- **Permission to use:** Jamie directed Codex to continue and prepare the proposed repository change.
- **Confidentiality boundary:** Paraphrased project feedback only; no employer, client or third-party operational information is included.

## Signal

- **What was reported or observed:** The project had treated OPERATE as the whole methodology, while Jamie intended a complete approach to managing and improving operations and progressing safely towards automation, AI and agents. The application asked for substantial user analysis but did not return meaningful in-context AI analysis, leaving its purpose and output unclear.
- **What the person was trying to achieve:** Establish the complete Operations Automated methodology and build its human-controlled evolution mechanism in tandem, then deliver useful analysis to individuals and organisations.
- **Why the current method or delivery was insufficient:** The approved overview described only the OPERATE lifecycle, and the product centred that lifecycle before the operational architecture and output model were defined.
- **Evidence supplied:** Direct founder feedback, repeated confusion during the Observe-Prioritise-Examine journey, repository descriptions and application code showing no embedded AI analysis service.
- **Evidence limitations and assumptions:** Evidence comes from the founder as first user; no independent-user or commercial validation exists. The usefulness of the proposed architecture remains to be tested.

## Triage

- **Affected material:** Founder Charter, project overview, governance, roadmap, OPERATE definition, principles, operational architecture, readiness, outputs, product direction and evolution workflow.
- **People potentially affected:** Jamie, future practitioners, operational teams and any later external users.
- **Frequency or related signals:** The absence of useful outputs and unclear project direction were raised repeatedly during private testing.
- **Consequence or urgency:** High. Continuing the existing route could build the wrong product against an incomplete methodology.
- **Related records:** [Provisional Operations Automated self-assessment](../pilots/operations-automated-self-assessment-001.md) and [v0.4 change proposal](../proposals/methodology-architecture-v0.4.md).
- **Recommended next action:** Review and decide the v0.4 proposal before further application development.
- **Owner and review date:** Jamie Peppard; review during draft PR #6.

## Disposition

- **State:** Proposed.
- **Resulting proposal:** v0.4 complete methodology architecture, evolution system and delivery reset in draft PR #6.
- **Reasoning:** The signal affects the project's purpose and architecture rather than a single interface defect. The methodology, evolution mechanism and delivery outputs must be corrected together.
- **Release or retained location:** Controlled repository branch `codex/methodology-architecture-v0.4` and [draft pull request #6](https://github.com/warpedmore-netizen/operations-automated/pull/6).
- **Outcome review trigger:** Jamie's decision, completion of the first evolution cycle and evidence from the first facilitated operational case.
