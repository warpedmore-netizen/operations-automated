# Daily founder methodology evolution loop

## Source and boundary

- **Source:** Jamie Peppard's direction after approval of v0.4.
- **Context:** Founder asked for the methodology and its update system to continue developing in tandem through regular challenge and feedback.
- **Permission to use:** Jamie explicitly asked Codex to create the next mechanism.
- **Confidentiality boundary:** Project direction only; no employer, client or third-party operational information.

## Signal

Jamie does not want the next step to depend on finding one external operational case. The immediate need is a living feedback cycle in which:

- Jamie can pose a methodology question and critique the answer produced by Operations Automated.
- AI sends a daily evidence-backed question or use case for a short founder response.
- AI retains, collates and analyses the feedback.
- Material insights become proposed methodology changes.
- Jamie receives an easy-to-understand way to assure the change before approval.
- Future external feedback channels can enter the same controlled process.

## Triage

- **Affected material:** Methodology evolution, governance, project operating instructions, roadmap, feedback records, decision assurance and future feedback connections.
- **Consequence:** High. Without a repeatable loop, the methodology may remain static or change through unstructured conversation without clear assurance.
- **Evidence limitation:** Founder need is clear, but daily question quality, response burden and change yield have not yet been tested.
- **Recommendation:** Run a read-only daily challenge at 08:00 UK time, process replies through the approved evolution workflow and review after seven completed responses.

## Current disposition

- **Automation:** `Daily methodology challenge` is active in Codex on Jamie's instruction.
- **Repository integration:** Proposed through v0.5.
- **External channels:** Not approved. Public sources may inform research, but the automation may not contact people, log into restricted services or collect confidential information.
- **Review trigger:** Seven completed founder responses or earlier if the questions are repetitive, burdensome, misleading or unsafe.
