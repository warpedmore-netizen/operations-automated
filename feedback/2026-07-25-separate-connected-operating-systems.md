# Separate the connected operating systems while retaining their learning loops

## Operational insight

Operations Automated should use its own tools to develop itself:

- Connected Governance creates and maintains the company's policies, controls, procedures, tests and findings.
- The AI Workbench supports the internal process for challenging and evolving the customer methodology.
- The approved internal methodology becomes the source for a distinct external reader edition.
- Scenario results, risks, comments, product use and operational outcomes create governed feedback.
- The Incident Manager RPG remains a standalone training product that may later provide authorised scenario evidence.

## Methodology and product feedback

Previous explanations risked treating company governance, internal methodology development and the customer methodology as one documentation system. They require distinct outputs, audiences and authorities even when they share evidence and learning.

Connected Governance should handle governing sources and operational findings. The Workbench should handle methodology challenges. Neither should silently update the other's authoritative meaning.

## Strongest contextual interpretation

“Synchronous” means that evidence and findings can be routed between systems with traceability. It does not mean a change propagates automatically. A scenario finding may justify several linked candidates, but each must retain its own authority and decision.

## Counter-test

A single undifferentiated knowledge base would make reuse easy, but it would also make it difficult to determine whether a statement is company policy, product behaviour, internal method-development practice or customer guidance. The resulting authority ambiguity outweighs the convenience.

## Disposition

**Propose clarification now.** Record the connected system architecture and test it by using Connected Governance to create the first Operations Automated business-governance Draft package. Do not change approved methodology meaning through this proposal.
