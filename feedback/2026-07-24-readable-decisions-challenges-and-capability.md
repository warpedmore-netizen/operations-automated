# Methodology and product feedback

## Source and boundary

- **Source or reference:** Direct feedback from Jamie Peppard during review of the Governed Feedback-to-Change Loop
- **Date received or observed:** 2026-07-24
- **User or operating context:** Founder review of the local Operations Automated Workbench and the developing Human-AI Collaboration Method
- **Permission to use:** Jamie explicitly asked for these considerations to be included in the current review and implementation
- **Confidentiality or data boundary:** Non-confidential Operations Automated project learning

## Signal

- **What was reported or observed:** Change review and approval must be easier for Jamie to read, with quick access to the exact GitHub change where appropriate. The Workbench should provide a clear button that asks AI to send one useful methodology challenge. Continuing development should test principles, whole-method AI suitability, deliberately manual work, the thinking required before automation, collaboration with development teams and the creation of lasting internal capability.
- **What the person was trying to achieve:** Make founder governance usable in practice while developing a broader, more balanced methodology through focused dialogue.
- **Why the current method or delivery was insufficient:** The governed loop retained the right evidence and controls, but its detailed proposal view still made the user assemble the decision from several technical sections. Challenge behaviour was available only through manually written prompts, and the proposed collaboration method did not yet state the manual-work, AI-usability or capability-building positions strongly enough.
- **Evidence limitations and assumptions:** These requirements currently reflect founder-led product use. Their transferability should be tested with other decision-makers, practitioners and delivery teams.

## Triage

- **Product change candidate:** Add a plain-English decision brief, prominent draft pull-request access and a Challenge Studio with focused one-question routes.
- **Methodology change candidate:** Propose an amendment to the approved Human-AI Collaboration Method with principles and tests for automation suitability, human and AI readability, manual work design, development-team collaboration and internal capability.
- **Approval state:** Not approved. Jamie's instruction authorises preparation in the current branch and draft pull request; it does not authorise release or merge.
- **Review trigger:** Founder use of the readable Decision Inbox and at least one challenge from each focus area.
