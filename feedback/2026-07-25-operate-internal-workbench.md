# Make Operate the connected internal workbench

## Source and boundary

- **Source:** Jamie Peppard's attached operating-model brief in the current Codex task.
- **Conversation reference:** Request to read and act on the brief beginning “Operate: Internal Workbench Operating Model”.
- **Information boundary:** Non-confidential Operations Automated product and methodology material.
- **Authority at intake:** Controlled product preparation is authorised. Methodology meaning, release, merge, publication, spending, consequential connections and delegated authority remain separate decisions.

## Jamie's judgement

The Workbench should become the place where operational work is captured, connected, prioritised and learned from. It should give a person one clear **My Work** view, make Cases, Requests and Tasks practical first, retain the wider Incident, Problem, Change, Risk, Finding and Continual Improvement model, and express the current brand direction consistently.

Jamie's brief also establishes these important product expectations:

- work should be captured in ordinary language and classified with assistance;
- the same real-world issue can create several related records without being forced through one rigid workflow;
- risk is cross-cutting rather than a separate operational universe;
- priority should reflect impact, urgency, risk, controls, dependencies and strategic value rather than simple age;
- Case should remain a flexible container for related work;
- the Operations Bible should provide the governed vocabulary and relationship model;
- Oppa Mate may recommend, route, update and automate within policy, but does not acquire approval authority; and
- the first release should prove one end-to-end loop before attempting the whole model.

## Recorded evidence

- The approved connected-work methodology already treats Cases, Requests, Tasks, Incidents, Problems, Changes, Risks, Controls, Decisions and learning as a connected network rather than isolated registers.
- The current Workbench already contains governed conversations, feedback, change decisions, brand review and Confluence controls, but those items are reached through separate areas.
- Before this proposal, the application did not provide one operational inbox, a durable Case/Request/Task record model, explainable operational priority or an in-product Operations Bible.
- The attached brief is founder product judgement. It is not independent-user demand evidence or an approval of every suggested record type and automation route.

## AI interpretation

The smallest coherent MVP is a work-control layer over the existing governed Workbench:

1. a unified **My Work** inbox;
2. explainable 80:20 priority and a short **Do Next** list;
3. durable Cases, Requests and Tasks with optional links;
4. the complete initial Operations Bible so later record types do not require a conflicting redesign;
5. explicit human confirmation for consequential approval and risk-acceptance states; and
6. existing Decision Inbox and Brand Review items surfaced without collapsing their authority controls.

The brief does not justify implementing autonomous execution, customer-facing collaboration, every scenario-testing function or all possible business-system connections now.

## Challenge tests

### Simplicity test

A universal mandatory workflow would reproduce the form-led complexity the methodology has already rejected. The proposal therefore recommends a type from ordinary language, allows correction and requires only a title to capture work.

### Authority test

A priority score can organise attention but cannot approve a Change, accept a Risk or decide methodology meaning. Approval and risk acceptance require Jamie's exact confirmation, and the score exposes its factors.

### Transfer test

The vocabulary must work for founder use now and later multi-role use. Owner, Case, journey, product and relationship fields are optional classifications rather than hidden permissions.

### Failure test

If the assistant classifies work incorrectly, the person can select another type and the retained activity distinguishes recommended from selected classification. If priority is wrong, the underlying factors remain visible and correctable.

### Scope test

Scenario testing, customer-journey analytics, sophisticated automation and external connections remain represented in the model but are not presented as completed MVP capability.

## What changed the AI assessment

The Workbench should no longer be framed primarily as a set of conversations and specialist governance areas. Those capabilities remain valuable, but the ordinary entry point should be the work a person must understand or act on now.

## Remaining uncertainty

- The proposed weightings have not been validated against real founder work over time.
- No independent user has tested the record definitions or capture language.
- The exact boundary between Case and project/programme management may need refinement after real use.
- The complete Operations Bible is an initial product dictionary, not approved methodology meaning.
- Brand Review feedback could not be queried from the usual local Workbench at the start of this task; retained repository feedback was applied, and no claim is made that the queue was empty.

## Disposition

**Product change candidate; prepare the bounded MVP now and accumulate use evidence.**

Prepare the unified inbox, Cases, Requests, Tasks, existing approvals and existing Brand Review loop end to end. Retain the wider connected model and dictionary without presenting all of it as implemented. Do not merge, publish, connect a new system or treat the proposed dictionary and weighting as approved until Jamie reviews the assurance pack and draft pull request.
