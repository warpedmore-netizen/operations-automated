# Mutual challenge and convergence

## Source and boundary

- **Source:** Jamie Peppard's reflection after the first founder challenge response was retained.
- **Context:** The first loop behaved too much like one question in and one answer out.
- **Permission to use:** Jamie explicitly asked for continuing questions, reverse tests and mutual challenge.
- **Confidentiality boundary:** Project direction and general reasoning only.

## Signal

The founder challenge loop should increase both human and AI capability. Jamie's judgement is valuable evidence, but it is not automatically true because it comes from the founder. AI analysis is not automatically true because it is computationally produced or evidence-rich.

Useful methodology learning may require several short exchanges:

1. AI presents an evidence-backed proposition or scenario.
2. Jamie provides operational judgement.
3. AI explains what it learned and what it still questions.
4. AI tests the answer using a reversed assumption, boundary case, different stakeholder, changed scale, contrary evidence or failure scenario.
5. Jamie refines, rejects or conditions the answer.
6. AI records convergence, unresolved disagreement or context-dependent rules.

The objective is not automatic agreement. It is a position that remains sensible after proportionate challenge, or a clear record of why reasonable disagreement remains.

## Challenge methods

Use one or more methods where they can materially change the conclusion:

- **Reverse test:** assume the proposed default is reversed and examine the consequence.
- **Boundary test:** apply the answer at very small, very large, low-risk or high-consequence scale.
- **Transfer test:** apply the same reasoning to a different operational setting.
- **Stakeholder inversion:** examine the answer from the viewpoint of another beneficiary or adversely affected person.
- **Disconfirming evidence:** look deliberately for credible evidence that would weaken the current conclusion.
- **Time-horizon test:** compare immediate value with capability, resilience or harm over time.
- **Failure and recovery test:** ask what happens when the preferred design fails or its assumptions stop holding.
- **Authority test:** distinguish a good recommendation from who is permitted to decide it.

## Controls

- Challenge should be proportionate and useful, not disagreement performed for its own sake.
- AI should state where Jamie's answer changed its assessment and where it still disagrees or lacks evidence.
- AI should challenge its own earlier inference as readily as it challenges Jamie.
- Before challenging, AI should present the strongest reasonable contextual interpretation and distinguish conversational generalisation from an intended universal rule.
- AI should challenge that strongest interpretation rather than an exaggerated literal version of shorthand.
- Use one focused counter-test at a time unless the issue is consequential enough to justify more.
- Jamie may pause, skip or stop a challenge without that choice becoming evidence for or against the proposition.
- Short replies, silence, repetition or fatigue must not be labelled convergence.
- Convergence means the reasoning has survived relevant tests, not that Jamie and AI use identical words.
- Material unresolved disagreement should remain visible in later assurance and approval.

## Disposition

**Material refinement to the proposed v0.5 evolution loop.**

Add an iterative challenge sequence to the proposed founder loop, daily check-in template, repository instructions and v0.5 success criteria. This changes the proposed feedback mechanism, not the approved v0.4 methodology.

## Retained lesson

Treat every answer as a hypothesis with human context, not a final truth. Seek convergence through useful challenge while retaining context, disagreement and human authority.
