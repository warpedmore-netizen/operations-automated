# Finish the Workbench as the operating surface

## Jamie's operational requirement

Jamie directed Codex to implement, rather than merely propose, one conversation-first operating surface for Operations Automated. Git must remain authoritative for methodology meaning; the operational database must retain work and its history; Oppa Mate must apply the method and prepare work; Codex must remain the external source-code builder; and Jamie must complete ordinary work and governed decisions in the Workbench.

He explicitly required that approval, merge, publication, risk acceptance and delegated authority must never be inferred.

## Strongest contextual interpretation

The requirement is not to collapse all records into one table or remove specialist expertise. It is to remove the practical fragmentation between capture, conversation, attention, source decisions, implementation handoff and review.

The smallest coherent interpretation is:

- one My Work attention layer and shared operational graph;
- source-backed adapters that preserve specialist history;
- one core Decision/Approval representation with scope-specific evidence;
- governed repository retrieval and conversation continuity;
- configurable record and Work Profile definitions; and
- a complete copyable Codex brief and structured return receipt.

## Evidence, inference and boundary

- **Recorded evidence:** the previous Workbench had separate conversations, Operate records, feedback, proposal, brand and publication queues; later responses did not reliably use retained conversation history; repository retrieval covered only a shallow subset.
- **Jamie's judgement:** another proposal would not solve the operational need.
- **AI inference:** additive SQLite migrations and source-backed projections are safer than replacing the current database or specialist stores.
- **Assumption:** a copy/paste Build Job is sufficient for this increment because no direct Codex connection is authorised or available.
- **Boundary:** this feedback authorises controlled implementation and draft-PR preparation. It does not approve the resulting product, merge, release, publication, a new connection or customer use.

## Proportionate challenge

- **Reverse test:** a single surface that hides distinct authority or evidence would be worse than specialist queues. The implementation therefore shares attention and controls while retaining source records and routes.
- **Failure test:** classifications, technical receipts and button availability cannot advance a consequential decision without the applicable exact confirmation.
- **Transfer test:** founder-specific names and local storage are suitable for the private pilot, not proof of a team or customer authority model.
- **Time-horizon test:** structured corrections and outcomes are retained for later evaluation; no fine-tuning or model training is justified now.
- **Dependency test:** a Build Job must not exist before its Change is approved for preparation; publication remains separate after merge.

## Disposition

**Propose product change now.** The issue affects the complete founder operating journey and can be implemented without changing approved methodology meaning. Real-use calibration and an explicit product release decision remain outstanding.
