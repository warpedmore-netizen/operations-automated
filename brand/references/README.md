# Initial brand reference set

These images are the founder-supplied visual source for Brand System v0.1. They correct the earlier assumption that the four-block prototype favicon was the intended Operations Automated logo.

| File | Dimensions | Role |
|---|---:|---|

| `initial-banner-wide-4200x700.png` | 4200 × 700 | High-resolution original banner and lock-up reference |
| `initial-linkedin-profile-cover-1584x396.png` | 1584 × 396 | Corrected LinkedIn personal-profile cover |

## Visual evidence

The references establish:

- a continuous OA loop symbol rather than a modular four-block mark;
- Obsidian and deep ink-blue backgrounds;
- a blue-to-electric-cyan movement accent;
- geometric, high-contrast, uppercase wordmark typography;
- a precise horizontal rule beneath the name;
- detailed connected or mesh-like background texture;
- a confident consultancy tone rather than a light editorial tone; and
- a short three-part strapline: **Automate. Autonomise. Empower.**

## Meaning and approval boundary

The references are design evidence, not automatic approval of every word or use.

- The OA loop mark is the working master symbol.
- The dark, precise and connected visual tone is the working direction.
- **Operations consultancy** remains a possible descriptor, not an approved limitation on the company or methodology.
- **Automate. Autonomise. Empower.** remains source wording pending a methodology-alignment decision. “Autonomise” must not imply that every operation should become autonomous or that human authority can be bypassed.
- The LinkedIn image is correctly sized for a personal-profile cover, but publication remains separately controlled.

LinkedIn currently recommends a 1584 × 396 px JPG or PNG of less than 8 MB for a personal profile cover: [LinkedIn profile cover guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217).

## Source handling

These are raster references. The generated transparent and monochrome mark assets are delivery derivatives, not a replacement for final vector source artwork. Before external brand release, obtain or recreate an approved vector master and check that its curves, spacing and colour stops match the founder-approved mark.
