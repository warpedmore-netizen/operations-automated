# Social profile templates

## LinkedIn personal-profile cover

Use [`linkedin-profile-cover-1584x396.png`](linkedin-profile-cover-1584x396.png) for Jamie's personal LinkedIn profile.

- Dimensions: 1584 × 396 px.
- Format: PNG.
- The lower-left area remains comparatively quiet because LinkedIn overlays the profile photograph there.
- Keep important text and the full mark away from the outer crop edges because display can vary with browser and screen size.
- Do not substitute LinkedIn company-page dimensions.

LinkedIn currently recommends a 1584 × 396 px JPG or PNG below 8 MB for a personal-profile cover: [official LinkedIn guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217).
