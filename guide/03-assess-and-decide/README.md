# 03 — Assess and decide

## Purpose and intended reader

This chapter turns operational understanding into a proportionate assessment, prioritised opportunity and governed decision. It is for a person deciding what should change, what evidence is still needed and what level of readiness or authority is justified.

## Questions this chapter answers

- Is the operation understandable, controlled and improvable?
- Is a work type ready for simplification, conventional automation, AI assistance or bounded agentic operation?
- Which opportunity matters most for the user-defined value and beneficiary?
- What evidence supports the assessment and how confident should the reader be?
- What trade-offs, risks, dependencies and alternatives must remain visible?
- Who can select the priority, accept consequence or authorise the next step?

## Topics

- Answerability and evidence sufficiency
- Outside-in assessment of the primary journey and supporting operational injection points
- Readiness as a profile rather than a universal maturity score
- Recorded fact, user judgement, AI inference, assumption and recommendation
- Category-first comparison where work types differ materially
- Prioritisation by value, consequence, evidence, effort, dependency, urgency and reversibility
- Options, trade-offs and credible no-change alternatives
- Decision authority, risk acceptance and retained reasoning

## Expected inputs

- Foundation and current-state outputs
- Primary journey, intended experience, affected people and feedback evidence
- Evidence supporting current performance and variation
- User-defined value priorities and minimum outcomes
- Risks, controls, dependencies and applicable obligations
- Opportunity options and a credible current-state alternative
- Feasibility, effort, reversibility and expected consequence
- Named decision and risk authority

## Outputs

- A proportionate operational assessment
- A journey-led view of which operational capabilities need improvement and why
- Evidence-based readiness profile by relevant work type
- Prioritised opportunities with visible trade-offs
- Missing evidence and smallest useful investigation
- Recommended target outcome or decision
- Retained decision, conditions and review trigger
- Explicit human control point

## Interfaces and hand-offs

| Interface | Decision contribution | Output or hand-off |
|---|---|---|
| Operational owner and affected users | Operational judgement and value priorities | Recommended priority and consequence |
| Finance and investment authority | Affordability, benefits and funding constraints | Evidence-led investment option |
| Risk, compliance, legal and safety | Specialist obligations and acceptance route | Risk context, residual exposure and decision need |
| Technology, architecture, data and security | Feasibility, readiness and technical risk | Bounded technology option and prerequisites |
| Change, project and delivery teams | Capacity, sequencing and dependency evidence | Authorised target outcome and implementation conditions |

## Known gaps

- Assessment depth and evidence thresholds need testing across different consequences and sectors.
- No approved confidence or evidence-quality scale exists.
- Prioritisation and sensitivity guidance remains incomplete.
- Readiness profiles need more worked examples, especially where different work types occupy different positions.
- Links between assessment, investment governance and portfolio selection need deeper guidance.
- A concise independent-user assessment guide has not been validated.
