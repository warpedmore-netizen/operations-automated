# 07 — Governance, assurance and review

## Purpose and intended reader

This chapter helps a reader retain authority, evidence, risk, approval and learning throughout operational work and methodology change. It is for accountable owners, decision-makers, risk and assurance roles, methodology maintainers and people preparing a consequential recommendation.

## Questions this chapter answers

- What needs a retained decision and who has authority?
- What evidence, alternatives, trade-offs and residual risk must be visible?
- How is a proposed change kept separate from approved guidance?
- What assurance is proportionate to consequence?
- How are feedback, failure and external change turned into controlled improvement?
- What is released, to whom, under what status and with what review trigger?

## Topics

- Artefact states and governance status
- Authority, accountability, delegation and risk acceptance
- Decision evidence and proportionality
- Methodology change assurance
- Feedback capture, triage, proposal, decision and release
- Human-controlled AI execution
- Review triggers, monitoring and retained no-change or rejection reasoning
- Publication, distribution and product synchronisation boundaries

## Expected inputs

- Trigger, source, permission and information boundary
- Current approved meaning and proposed or operational decision
- Evidence, judgement, inference, assumptions and uncertainty
- Affected people, practices, products, controls and records
- Credible alternatives and consequence of no change
- Named authority and scope of decision
- Validation, recovery and review conditions

## Outputs

- Proportionate decision or assurance record
- Explicit approval, revision, deferral, rejection or no-change disposition
- Conditions, residual risk and review trigger
- Traceable change proposal and affected-system view
- Versioned release or retained proposal
- Clear statement of what remains unapproved
- Outcome observation and retained learning

## Interfaces and hand-offs

| Interface | Governance contribution | Hand-off |
|---|---|---|
| Accountable operational owner | Purpose, ownership and consequence | Decision packet and retained conditions |
| Risk, compliance, audit, legal, safety and security | Specialist challenge and assurance | Evidence, residual exposure and acceptance need |
| Finance, procurement and leadership | Spend, commitment and contractual authority | Options, value, dependency and decision boundary |
| Product, technology and delivery | Implementation, migration and release evidence | Approved meaning, controls and version |
| Methodology maintainer | Consistency, traceability and release preparation | Feedback, proposal, checks and explicit decision |
| Users and affected people | Outcome evidence, feedback and challenge | Visible change, limitation and review route |

## Known gaps

- The governance document itself remains proposed.
- No complete operating-governance practice defines forums, thresholds, delegation reviews and assurance independence.
- Publication, withdrawal, migration and version-distribution guidance remains incomplete.
- No standard maps operational authority across an individual, team and organisation.
- Product and methodology release synchronisation needs tested implementation.
- Outcome review has not yet completed a full feedback-to-release-to-observation cycle.
