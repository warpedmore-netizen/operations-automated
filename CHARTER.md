# Operations Automated Founder Charter

## Purpose

Operations Automated exists to help people understand, govern and improve how work happens and to use automation, AI and agents without surrendering human judgement, authority or accountability.

It turns operational experience into a practical, repeatable and evolving methodology that can support an individual activity, a team process, a service or an organisation-wide operating system.

## Intended users

- Individuals improving a recurring activity, process or system
- Operations and service managers
- Operational excellence and transformation teams
- Incident, change, resilience and continuity practitioners
- Process owners and team leaders
- Risk, control, quality and audit practitioners
- Automation, AI and agentic-system practitioners
- People improving cross-functional work without a formal operations title

## Problem

Operational work often depends on undocumented knowledge, inherited processes, disconnected tools, weak handovers and individual heroics. It spans services, cases, requests, incidents, problems, changes, audits and other work types that are frequently managed in isolation even though their outcomes are connected.

Traditional frameworks can become too rigid, theoretical or slow to reflect how organisations and technology change. At the same time, pressure to adopt automation, AI and agents can cause organisations to scale unclear work, weak data and uncontrolled decisions.

Operations Automated will provide an understandable system that combines evidence, criteria and controlled judgement; improves the operation as a whole; establishes readiness before delegation; and continually learns from outcomes.

## Core beliefs

1. Start with purpose, not process.
2. Observe reality before redesigning it.
3. Use evidence, then apply judgement.
4. Design for people who are new, rushed, distracted or under pressure.
5. Simplify before automating.
6. Automate repetition, not accountability.
7. Keep humans where context, empathy or consequence matters.
8. Make ownership and decisions visible.
9. Controls should support safe work, not ceremonial compliance.
10. Test exceptions and failure, not only the happy path.
11. Measure outcomes rather than activity for its own sake.
12. Define value before deciding what better means.
13. Make failure safe enough to reveal useful learning quickly.
14. Treat operations as connected systems rather than isolated process maps.
15. Operational readiness comes before automation, AI or agentic readiness.
16. Give agents only explicit, limited and observable authority with recovery routes.
17. Everything evolves, including this methodology.

## Human responsibility

People retain responsibility for:

- Purpose and intended value
- Context and operational knowledge
- Judgement and empathy
- Leadership and ownership
- Authority boundaries and permitted delegation
- Acceptance of risk and consequence
- Approval of material methodology meaning and release

AI may question, organise, analyse, recommend and execute authorised tasks. It does not become the unaccountable owner of an operation or acquire authority merely because it can perform an action.

## Method structure

The Operations Automated methodology combines:

- Operational lenses for understanding connected work
- A readiness path from operational basics through automation, AI and agents
- OPERATE as the improvement and implementation cycle
- A useful-output contract for facilitated and product delivery
- An AI-managed, human-controlled methodology evolution system

The components should be applied proportionately and developed together through evidence from real use.

## Boundaries

Operations Automated must not become:

- Automation or AI consultancy disguised as a neutral methodology
- A rigid certification bureaucracy or unexplained maturity score
- A collection of templates without operational reasoning
- A system that treats efficiency or technology adoption as more important than people, customers, safety or user-defined value
- An autonomous authority permitted to publish, spend, contact people, accept risk or change controls without defined permission
- A copy of an employer's intellectual property or internal frameworks
- A claim that general methodology replaces applicable legal, safety, security, ethical or specialist obligations

## Founder authority

During the founder-controlled phase, Jamie Peppard has final approval over methodology content, publication, external communication, expenditure, security changes, consequential connections and delegation of authority.

Authority may later be delegated only through explicit, limited and reversible rules supported by monitoring and an audit trail.

## Evolution

The methodology evolves through evidence, practitioner feedback, operational outcomes, research and structured challenge. AI may capture signals, identify contradictions, draft changes, run checks and notify the authorised human. Material changes require human review under the governance model.

The methodology should be applied to Operations Automated itself so that the project learns from its own design, delivery and failures.
