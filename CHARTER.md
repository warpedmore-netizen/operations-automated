# Operations Automated Founder Charter

## Purpose

Operations Automated exists to help people understand how work really happens, improve it deliberately and use automation and AI without surrendering human judgement or accountability.

It turns operational experience into practical, repeatable and evolving methods that can be used by Operations Managers and adapted by other roles responsible for improving work.

## Intended users

- Operations and service managers
- Operational excellence and transformation teams
- Incident, change, resilience and continuity practitioners
- Process owners and team leaders
- Automation and AI practitioners
- People improving cross-functional work without a formal operations title

## Problem

Operational work often depends on undocumented knowledge, inherited processes, disconnected tools and individual heroics. Traditional frameworks can become too rigid, theoretical or slow to reflect how organisations and technology change.

Operations Automated will provide an understandable system that combines evidence, criteria and controlled judgement; simplifies work before automating it; and continually learns from outcomes.

## Core beliefs

1. Start with purpose, not process.
2. Observe reality before redesigning it.
3. Use evidence, then apply judgement.
4. Design for people who are new, rushed, distracted or under pressure.
5. Simplify before automating.
6. Automate repetition, not accountability.
7. Keep humans where context, empathy or consequence matters.
8. Make ownership and decisions visible.
9. Controls should support safe work, not ceremonial compliance.
10. Test exceptions and failure, not only the happy path.
11. Measure outcomes rather than activity for its own sake.
12. Define value before deciding what “better” means.
13. Make failure safe enough to reveal useful learning quickly.
14. Everything evolves, including this methodology.

## Human responsibility

People retain responsibility for:

- Purpose
- Context and operational knowledge
- Judgement
- Empathy
- Leadership
- Acceptance of risk and consequence

AI may question, organise, analyse, recommend and execute authorised tasks. It does not become the unaccountable owner of an operation.

## Boundaries

Operations Automated must not become:

- Automation consultancy disguised as a neutral methodology
- A rigid certification bureaucracy
- A collection of templates without operational reasoning
- A system that treats efficiency as more important than people, customers or safety
- An autonomous authority permitted to publish, spend, contact people or change controls without defined permission
- A copy of an employer's intellectual property or internal frameworks

## Founder authority

During the founder-controlled phase, Jamie Peppard has final approval over methodology content, publication, external communication, expenditure, security changes and delegation of authority.

Authority may later be delegated only through explicit, limited and reversible rules supported by monitoring and an audit trail.

## Evolution

The methodology evolves through evidence, practitioner feedback, operational outcomes, research and structured challenge. AI may draft changes and identify contradictions, but material changes require human review under the governance model.
