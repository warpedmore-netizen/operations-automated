# Private OPERATE workspace MVP

## Product outcome

Give a problem solver a private workspace that turns an unclear operational problem into a governed next action without requiring developer knowledge or surrendering human control.

The workspace is the control and evidence surface. AI may help examine, draft and recommend, but the application makes value, state, authority and retained learning visible.

## First user and first job

Jamie is the first user. The first job is to take a real, non-confidential operational problem from initial description through a proportionate OPERATE cycle while retaining the decisions and learning needed for the next cycle.

## MVP capabilities

- Create and retain one private operational workspace in the browser
- Define the problem, people affected and owner
- Complete a user-defined value matrix
- Work through all seven OPERATE stages
- Calculate and explain the next governed action
- Require named human approval at consequential stage gates
- Retain a timestamped activity record
- Export the workspace as Markdown or JSON
- Import a previously exported JSON workspace
- Produce a structured brief that can be given to Codex for AI assistance

## Governance behaviour

- The engine may identify missing evidence and recommend the next action.
- It may not approve value priorities, a redesign, automation, test acceptance or retained learning on behalf of a human.
- An approval records who approved it and when.
- The MVP does not publish, contact people, spend money, connect services or execute operational automation.
- Browser persistence is a convenience, not an authoritative business record or secure multi-user data store.

## Deliberate non-goals

- User accounts or multi-user collaboration
- Cloud or database storage
- External AI or business-system connections
- Automated publication or methodology changes
- Payments, subscriptions or commercial licensing
- A claim that the product provides legal, safety or professional advice

## Validation measures

- Jamie can start and progress a problem without developer assistance.
- The application always explains the next action and whether AI or a human owns it.
- Consequential stage transitions cannot occur without named human approval.
- A complete export explains the value, evidence, decisions, approvals and learning.
- The workflow reveals useful friction before any external connection is requested.

## Product and revenue hypothesis

“Passive income” is treated as a goal for scalable recurring revenue, not a promise that the product will require no support, governance or continued improvement.

A credible product ladder to validate is:

1. **Open guidance:** selected public methodology content and a lightweight problem diagnostic build trust and demand.
2. **Individual workspace:** a paid self-service product helps practitioners run and retain governed OPERATE cycles.
3. **Team workspace:** a higher-value product adds shared evidence, roles, approval routes, reporting and controlled integrations.
4. **Business licence:** organisations pay for managed deployment, governance controls and methodology updates.
5. **Optional enablement:** training, accreditation or partner delivery may supplement product revenue without becoming the core dependency.

Pricing, distribution and publication remain unapproved hypotheses. They should be tested with real users after the private MVP demonstrates useful outcomes.

## Decision gates before external use

- Validate the workflow on real non-confidential problems
- Define data classification, retention, deletion and recovery requirements
- Decide whether AI is required inside the product and what information it may receive
- Evaluate authentication, hosting, privacy, security and applicable legal obligations
- Test willingness to use and willingness to pay
- Obtain Jamie's explicit approval for external publication and commercial activity
