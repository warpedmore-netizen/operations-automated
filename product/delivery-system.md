# Operations Automated delivery system

The delivery system is how an individual, team or organisation receives value from the Operations Automated methodology.

It should help the user understand and improve an operation, determine its justified automation, AI and agentic readiness, design a solution, implement it safely and retain learning. It must produce useful analysis and working artefacts rather than merely collect answers.

## Learning from the first application experiment

The approved private OPERATE Workspace MVP 0.1 demonstrated that a local record, stage flow and human approval gates can be implemented. Private testing also exposed a more important limitation: the application does not contain an AI analysis service and mainly reorganises information supplied by the user.

The result can feel like a form asking the user to perform the methodology themselves. A copied prompt is not an adequate substitute for a product returning analysis in context.

This proposal therefore recommends:

- Parking the current application as a retained learning experiment
- Preserving its code and evidence without treating it as the target product
- Not merging the unapproved progressive-interface experiment into `main`
- Validating the complete methodology and output contract before rebuilding a user interface

Parking is not deletion. Workspace records, approval gates, version visibility, exports and grouped work may later support a product once they are attached to genuine analysis and a validated user journey.

## Immediate delivery MVP

The immediate MVP is a facilitated Operations Automated service using Jamie, Codex and the controlled repository:

1. A user describes a real, non-confidential operational need in ordinary language.
2. AI frames the scope, value, people, authority and evidence boundary.
3. AI analyses the operation through the relevant operational lenses.
4. AI returns findings, uncertainty, readiness, options, trade-offs and a recommended next action.
5. The authorised human provides context, decisions and approval where consequence matters.
6. AI develops the operational design, implementation route and retained artefacts.
7. Feedback about the method enters the methodology evolution system.

This allows the method and evolution system to be tested before committing to a particular application design or infrastructure.

## Future product capabilities

A future private product should be considered only after the facilitated MVP demonstrates useful outputs. Its likely capabilities include:

- Conversational and guided intake for people without methodology training
- Progressive analysis conforming to the methodology output contract
- Operational system and dependency mapping
- Evidence-based readiness profiles
- Prioritised opportunities and implementation roadmaps
- Human approval, authority and risk-acceptance gates
- Traceable source, inference, uncertainty and recommendation views
- Retained workspaces, artefacts, decisions and learning
- Versioned methodology content and release notes
- Proportionate feedback capture linked to the evolution system
- Secure, controlled connections to approved AI and business services

## Product boundary

The delivery product is not:

- A questionnaire that labels collected text as analysis
- A universal automation recommender
- An autonomous operations authority
- A replacement for accountable operational leadership or specialist obligations
- A certification score presented as proof of safety or performance

## Validation before rebuilding

Run at least two real, non-confidential facilitated cases that differ in scope or work type. Confirm that:

- The methodology creates a useful operational picture and recommendation
- The user receives value throughout the journey
- The readiness path distinguishes sensible stopping points from justified automation, AI or agentic progression
- The process deals with cross-team dependencies, risk, exceptions and human judgement
- The retained output is useful for implementation and later review
- Feedback can produce a controlled methodology proposal

Only then define the smallest product that improves delivery without removing the reasoning that made the service useful.

## Commercial direction

Commercial options remain hypotheses. A later delivery ladder may include open guidance, paid individual analysis, team workspaces, business licences and optional enablement. Research into users, demand, willingness to pay, support cost and distribution should follow evidence that the methodology creates repeatable value.

No pricing, external publication, sales activity, hosting or external connection is approved by this proposal.
