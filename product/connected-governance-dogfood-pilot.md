# Connected Governance business dogfood pilot

## Purpose

Use Connected Governance as the first structured customer of its own service by creating Operations Automated's internal business-governance foundation.

The pilot tests product behaviour. It does not approve the generated documents as company policy or change the customer methodology.

## Starting profile

| Field | Dogfooding value |
|---|---|
| Organisation | Operations Automated |
| Sector | Operations management methodology and governance technology |
| Stage | Founder-led private internal validation |
| Services | Customer methodology, AI Workbench, Connected Governance and governed publication |
| Dependencies | Authorised roles, GitHub, private Workbench, Confluence Cloud, OpenAI and Atlassian |
| Desired outcome | Create minimum role-based company governance while keeping methodology meaning separate |

## Candidate foundation

The first assessment may propose:

1. Business Governance Framework;
2. Roles and Delegated Authority Standard;
3. Human-led AI and Automation Policy;
4. Information Handling and Confidentiality Policy;
5. Risk, Control and Assurance Framework;
6. Connections and Credential Policy;
7. Documentation, Change and Publication Policy;
8. Continuity and Recovery Policy;
9. Finding and Improvement Procedure; and
10. Governance Scenario Test Standard.

Each document remains `proposed`, names organisational roles rather than an individual and targets **Internal / Draft**.

## First controlled route

1. Select the Operations Automated dogfooding context.
2. Inspect and amend the organisation and authority profiles.
3. Review the current-document inventory and known gaps.
4. Accept, defer or reject the proposed foundation.
5. Assemble substantive candidate documents.
6. Read each document and include suitable candidates in the Draft hand-off.
7. Commit the proposed source and inspect the Confluence comparison.
8. Publish a conflict-free Draft-only plan through the existing private Workbench connection.
9. Review the human reading result.
10. Classify findings as company-governance, product, methodology or explanation issues.

The first implementation prepares the structured candidate package and Draft hand-off. It does not silently reuse a local credential in the hosted product. The existing private Workbench remains the controlled publication boundary while the products are separate.

## Scenario feedback

After the foundation is readable, run one bounded governance scenario. The first scenario should test whether a material product or connection decision is correctly routed between:

- Governance Authority;
- Methodology Authority;
- Document Owner;
- Control Owner;
- AI drafting roles; and
- Publication Operator.

The result may create policy, procedure, control, product or methodology candidates. It cannot update any of them automatically.

## Success measures

- The tester reaches the Operations Automated profile without retyping its context.
- The interface distinguishes company governance from methodology meaning.
- Candidate documents contain usable prose rather than document names only.
- Every candidate declares proposed status, authority, source and Draft destination.
- No candidate names an individual as the standing role.
- The existing private Confluence credential is not exposed or duplicated.
- Draft publication remains conflict-protected and does not promote content to Live.
- At least one retained finding is routed to the correct system.

## Boundaries

- Private internal dogfooding only.
- No company policy is approved by this pilot.
- No methodology meaning changes.
- No automatic retrieval of regulatory sources, comments or scenario results.
- No new external connection or permission.
- No Live, external or customer publication.
- No deletion or silent overwrite.
