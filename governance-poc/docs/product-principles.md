# Product principles

1. Structured records are canonical; documents are views.
2. Traceability precedes polish.
3. Source wording, interpretation and AI suggestion remain distinguishable.
4. AI proposes; named humans decide.
5. Approved records are immutable; change creates a version.
6. Scenarios and incidents test the governance design.
7. Evidence, uncertainty, ownership and authority remain visible.
8. The system never claims compliance automatically.
9. Change cadence follows risk, evidence and review need—not product theatre; annual review may be proportionate, while urgent findings can be handled quickly.
10. Product learning may inform methodology proposals but cannot silently change the methodology.
