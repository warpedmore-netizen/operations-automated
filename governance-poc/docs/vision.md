# Vision

Create a reusable service, informed by Operations Automated, that helps users develop and maintain living operational documentation. Regulations, interpretations, controls, policy requirements, procedures, tests, incidents, evidence, findings and releases are connected, versioned records; documents are generated views. The connected records form a governed knowledge base that later AI can help monitor and reason over without acquiring approval authority.

This is not a methodology update. Service findings can be retained as evidence for the separate controlled methodology-evolution loop. The product optimises for dependable, rapid updates when evidence or periodic review requires them; it does not impose false real-time urgency on documents commonly reviewed annually.
