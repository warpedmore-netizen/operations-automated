# Product roadmap

- **M0 — proof:** complete fictional browser workflow and safeguard tests.
- **M1 — validate:** facilitated demonstrations; test whether traceability, living documentation and a reusable knowledge base reduce reconstruction and maintenance effort.
- **M2 — persist:** typed service, relational schema, migrations, authenticated approvals and access controls.
- **M3 — assure:** threat model, accessibility, privacy, retention, backup/recovery and independent governance testing.
- **M4 — connect selectively:** approved AI and publishing/action adapters with reconciliation.
- **M5 — pilot:** bounded private organisational pilot using non-production or authorised data.

Each transition is a decision, not an automatic consequence of technical completion.

Service findings may be packaged as evidence for Operations Automated's controlled evolution process. Product evidence and methodology authority remain separate.
