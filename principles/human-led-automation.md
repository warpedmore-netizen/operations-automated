# Human-led Automation

Technology may execute work, surface evidence and recommend action. People provide its purpose, context, judgement, empathy and leadership.

## Automation decision

Before automating, establish:

- The purpose and desired outcome
- How often the work occurs and what effort it consumes
- Whether the current process is understood
- The frequency and consequence of exceptions
- Which decisions are rules and which require judgement
- Required controls, evidence and recovery routes
- Who owns the automated outcome

## Prohibited shortcut

Do not automate an unclear process merely to make the confusion happen faster.

## Human involvement

Human involvement should be retained where:

- Consequences are material or difficult to reverse
- Context changes the correct decision
- Empathy and communication materially affect the outcome
- Evidence is incomplete or contradictory
- Risk acceptance or accountability is required

## Monitoring

Every material automation should have an owner, success measure, failure signal, recovery method, review trigger and accessible audit history.
