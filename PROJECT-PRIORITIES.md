# Current MoSCoW priorities and outstanding work

## Current agreed position

- **Approved baseline:** v0.5 for internal validation only.
- **Active methodology proposal:** v0.6 actionable decision aids in draft PR #9; not approved or authorised for merge.
- **Latest proposed additions:** activation and first use, the Human-AI Collaboration Method, and the current visual synthesis; none is approved.
- **Separate service proof of concept:** operational governance as code in draft PR #8; built but not approved or merged.
- **Older pilot draft:** change-workflow evidence in draft PR #3; not approved and now requires a deliberate keep, supersede or close decision.
- **Application position:** the original application remains parked as learning. No production application, external release or commercial offer is approved.

This register states the shared working priorities. It does not itself approve proposed methodology, product or commercial changes.

## Must

These are required to preserve the intended method or unblock the next justified decision.

1. **Keep AI-led work human-controlled.** Jamie retains methodology meaning, consequential authority, release, merge, publication, spending, contact and risk-acceptance decisions.
2. **Return useful value, not just questions or polished conversation.** Give the user an answer where justified or a practical route towards one where it is not.
3. **Default to the shortest usable output.** A simple question should receive a simple guide, checklist or worked structure. A larger workbook or assessment is optional depth, not the default.
4. **Explain how to use every artefact.** The user should know what to enter, what the result means, what it cannot decide and what to do next without needing technical knowledge.
5. **Define value and categorise materially different work before scoring or improving it.** Do not hide mixed work, assumptions or priorities inside one number.
6. **Keep evidence, uncertainty, obligations and human authority visible.** A score or AI recommendation cannot create evidence, approval, safety or compliance.
7. **Complete a second materially different, non-confidential facilitated case.** Select the smallest useful output from the need rather than assuming another spreadsheet.
8. **Simplify the first-case output before treating it as validated.** Retain the comprehensive workbook as a deeper prototype; produce and test a short first-use version.
9. **Use an assurance pack and explicit Jamie decision before approving or merging v0.6.** One founder-reviewed prototype is insufficient.
10. **Resolve the three open draft PRs deliberately.** Do not merge PR #3, #8 or #9 merely because the work is technically complete.
11. **Prove activation and first use.** A created artefact is incomplete until its intended user can reach and begin using it, or the blocker and recovery route are explicit.
12. **Govern the human-AI collaboration.** AI should reconstruct context, do the analysis, choose the smallest useful representation, challenge proportionately and retain material learning without acquiring authority.

## Should

These materially improve usefulness, validation or control but do not all block the next case.

1. Test the simplified aid with someone who did not help design it.
2. Measure time to first useful outcome, confusion, completion effort and need for facilitation.
3. Build a small library of simple checklists, one-page guides and optional deeper templates from validated cases.
4. Continue the daily and ad-hoc methodology challenge when capacity permits.
5. Validate terminology outside service-management settings and with different user types.
6. Develop practical guidance for assurance independence, silent harm, recovery, record proportionality and human capability.
7. Decide which governance-POC capabilities are useful evidence for the future product before merging any of them.
8. Define the smallest product journey only after repeated outputs prove useful: Ask, optional deeper assessment, retained evidence and human decision.
9. Validate one narrow buyer, problem and paid outcome before building a commercial product.
10. Keep versions, sources, assumptions and approval states visible in every reusable artefact.
11. Pilot the proposed collaboration method across different tasks, formats, disagreements and control points.
12. Measure first-use success rather than relying only on construction tests.

## Could

These are possible later options, subject to evidence, priority and separate approval.

1. Native Google Sheets, Excel, Word or PDF outputs.
2. Reusable formulae and scoring tools where comparison genuinely benefits from calculation.
3. Multi-workspace and grouped-project views with shared principles and governance.
4. A versioned operational knowledge base with connected policies, procedures, controls, risks and decisions.
5. Confluence, Jira, Notion or other publication adapters.
6. Cloud scheduling and mobile, email, Teams or other notifications.
7. Consented feedback forms, usage analytics and a structured evidence store.
8. A focused guide, practitioner toolkit, facilitated assessment or later subscription workspace.
9. LinkedIn content and other controlled public explanations based on non-confidential hypothetical cases.
10. Team collaboration, reporting, licensing and delivery-partner models after the individual value is proven.

## Won't (for now)

These are outside the current approved boundary, not necessarily permanent rejections.

1. Rebuild, deploy or sell the application before the useful outputs and journey are validated.
2. Require users to complete the entire methodology or an oppressive workbook for a simple question.
3. Treat one formula, score, framework or maturity grade as a universal answer.
4. Assume cost, speed, efficiency, AI adoption or headcount reduction is always the primary value.
5. Use confidential employer, client, customer, employee or third-party information in the repository or tests.
6. Let AI approve methodology, accept operational risk, authorise spending, publish externally or contact people autonomously.
7. Claim FCA, PRA, ITIL, NIST, ISO or other compliance, certification or professional assurance that has not been performed.
8. Connect Google Drive, analytics, messaging, payment, business or customer systems without a separate purpose, data, security, cost and recovery decision.
9. Sell an unchanged document as a continuing subscription without recurring value.
10. Merge open prototypes because they exist; evidence and an explicit decision must come first.
