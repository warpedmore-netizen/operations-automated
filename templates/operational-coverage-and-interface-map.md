# Operational coverage and interface map

## How to use this tool

Use this template to:

1. identify which operational capabilities are material to an outcome;
2. record how deeply the available methodology or local handbook covers them;
3. expose important connections with other capabilities; and
4. retain the smallest useful action for a material gap.

Do not complete every row at maximum depth. Mark a capability not material only with a short reason where overlooking it could change the outcome.

The coverage levels describe the available guidance, not the maturity or performance of the operation:

- **Identified:** recognised but no usable guidance;
- **Outlined:** principal questions, interfaces and outputs described;
- **Usable:** repeatable method, roles, controls and artefacts available;
- **Validated:** useful across materially different cases with retained evidence; and
- **Publishable:** approved and maintainable for an external audience.

Use the [operational coverage model](../methodology/operational-coverage-model.md) for definitions and the [cross-functional interface model](../methodology/cross-functional-interfaces.md) for interface guidance.
