# AI and agent operational assurance and value case 001

## Purpose

Use one bounded, non-confidential operation to test whether RP-01 and RP-02 create a better operational decision without excessive record burden. The case is not yet selected, and no result is implied.

The preferred case is one named AI-enabled workflow or internal AI-built service with a real owner, users, baseline and review decision. If it performs agentic actions, both proposals apply directly. If it does not, RP-01 must show why AI-ready controls are sufficient rather than forcing an agent profile.

## Minimum founder input

Jamie supplies only information that is authorised and non-confidential:

1. the operation or workflow in ordinary language;
2. the primary person and outcome;
3. what the AI or agent may prepare, influence or execute;
4. the material failure or harm to avoid; and
5. the available baseline/cost evidence and decision owner.

AI returns a provisional assessment before asking for further detail. No employer, client, customer, employee or third-party confidential information is required.

## Scope and exclusion

Included: journey, outcome, owner, delegation, runtime assurance, baseline, full cost, value, failure, recovery and the next governed decision.

Excluded: production connection, new provider access, credentials, personal monitoring, external contact, spending, risk acceptance, methodology approval, product build and publication.

| 1. Frame | Follow the primary journey, define outcome, scope, owner and minimum service | Case boundary and value statement |

| 3. Delegation | Apply RP-01 to actual goals, identities, permissions, actions and human control | Agentic/AI-ready decision and assurance gaps |
| 4. Value | Apply RP-02 to lifecycle, full cost, outcome unit and transferred work | Decision-ready ledger |
| 5. Failure | Test normal, exception, adversarial, degraded, unauthorised and stop/recovery conditions proportionately | Observed control and recovery evidence |

| 7. Learn | Record confusion, burden, missing guidance and outcome review trigger | Proposed-guide disposition |

## RP-01 acceptance evidence

- Another practitioner can identify the real owner and represented authority.
- Material action can be reconstructed from proportionate runtime evidence.
- Approval-gated and prohibited actions are technically and operationally distinguishable.
- The external stop and recovery route succeeds or the blocker is explicit.
- The profile reveals an agent-specific decision rather than duplicating ordinary controls.
- Record burden remains proportionate to consequence.

## RP-02 acceptance evidence

- Material inclusion and discovery rules are understandable.
- Baseline and AI-enabled outcomes are comparable with stated uncertainty.
- Full cost per accepted outcome is usable or a defensible range/limitation is recorded.
- Quality, harm and transferred work remain visible beside cost.
- The ledger exposes a decision that adoption or token totals would miss.
- The owner can maintain the minimum record.

## Measures

- time to first useful finding;
- clarification effort and inaccessible language;
- number of fields removed or added after use;
- material authority, failure or value gaps exposed;
- whether the evidence changes a decision;
- owner confidence with reasons, not a maturity score; and
- activation: whether the intended user can begin without repository or developer knowledge.
