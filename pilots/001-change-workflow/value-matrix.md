# Plain-language change workflow value matrix

## Scope

- **Problem or opportunity:** Turn Jamie's plain-language direction into controlled methodology changes without requiring developer knowledge or weakening human control.
- **User-defined value proposition:** Jamie can lead as a problem solver while AI manages routine repository execution and makes the current position, evidence and next decision understandable.
- **Decision required:** Is the approved workflow useful enough to continue into a second method pilot before product development?
- **People affected:** Jamie initially; future practitioners and contributors if the approach is later published.

## Matrix

| Desired outcome | Beneficiary | Form of value | Priority | Minimum acceptable outcome | Constraints | Decision authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Understand the current position and next step without developer knowledge | Jamie | Clarity and accessibility | Must | Every milestone states where the project is, what happens next and what Jamie needs to decide | Plain language; no assumption of Git or GitHub knowledge | Jamie defines whether the explanation is sufficient |
| Move useful work forward without Jamie managing repository mechanics | Jamie | Momentum and reduced effort | Must | AI prepares branches, content, checks and draft pull requests within the agreed scope | AI must not infer authority for consequential actions | Jamie sets direction and boundaries; AI chooses routine implementation details |
| Preserve explicit human control | Jamie and people affected by future use | Safety, accountability and trust | Must | Approval, publication, connections, spending and consequential automation have visible human gates | Governance, legal, safety, ethical and human obligations remain applicable | Jamie during the founder-controlled phase |
| Retain evidence and learning across conversations | The project and future users | Traceability and learning | High | Material decisions, pilots and lessons are recorded in the repository | No confidential employer, client or third-party information | Jamie approves authoritative meaning; AI maintains records |
| Avoid building the wrong product too early | Jamie and future users | Effectiveness and adaptability | High | At least two method pilots inform the minimum viable product definition | Product choices remain hypotheses until supported by evidence | Jamie approves product scope and material commitments |

## Trade-offs

| Conflicting outcomes | Credible options | Effect on beneficiaries and value | Authorised decision-maker | Decision and reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speed versus human control | AI acts autonomously; Jamie operates every tool; AI executes routine work with explicit consequence gates | Full autonomy weakens control; manual operation creates avoidable burden; gated execution preserves momentum and accountability | Jamie | Use AI-led execution with explicit human approval for authoritative meaning and consequential action |
| Simple communication versus complete traceability | Put everything in chat; present all technical detail; retain detail in the repository and summarise decisions plainly | Chat alone loses durable evidence; full detail obscures decisions; layered communication serves both needs | Jamie | Keep authoritative detail in the repository and give Jamie a concise milestone handoff |
| Build an application now versus validate the method first | Begin software immediately; continue documentation indefinitely; run bounded pilots before defining the product | Immediate build risks automating untested assumptions; indefinite design delays learning; pilots create decision evidence | Jamie | Complete two method pilots before approving a product scope |

## Evidence and assumptions

- **Evidence used:** The completed v0.2 and v0.3 repository workflows; Jamie's repeated requests for a clear statement of the current position, project plan and required action; the approved governance model.
- **Assumptions requiring validation:** A consistent milestone handoff will provide sufficient clarity; two varied pilots will expose the most important method gaps; repository records can remain useful without becoming burdensome.
- **Information still missing:** A second real operational problem; future user needs beyond Jamie; required data handling and deployment constraints.

## Review

- **Review trigger:** Completion of Pilot 2 or evidence that the current workflow is confusing, unsafe or disproportionately heavy.
- **Review owner:** Jamie Peppard.
- **Next review date, if needed:** At the end of Pilot 2.
