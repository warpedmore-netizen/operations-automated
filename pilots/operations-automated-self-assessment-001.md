# Provisional Operations Automated self-assessment

## Purpose and evidence boundary

This assessment was created while v0.4 was proposed and is retained as provisional evidence under the approved internal-validation baseline. Its purpose is to test whether the architecture returns a useful operational picture, exposes gaps and produces a governed next action.

It uses the controlled repository, Jamie's direction and feedback in the project conversation, and observed behaviour from the private application test. It does not use external-user, commercial or market evidence. It is a draft assessment, not approval of v0.4.

## Direction and boundaries

- **Operation:** Develop, govern, validate, release and eventually deliver the Operations Automated methodology.
- **Problem:** The project narrowed the methodology to OPERATE and started building a stage-based application before defining the wider operational system, the methodology's usable outputs or its controlled evolution mechanism.
- **Intended outcome:** A practical whole-operations methodology that helps individuals, teams and organisations improve work and progress safely towards justified automation, AI and agentic operation.
- **Scope:** Methodology content, repository governance, AI-assisted change workflow, private delivery experiments and feedback-to-release flow.
- **Out of scope:** External publication, customer contact, confidential operational data, commercial commitments, production hosting and external service connections.
- **People affected:** Jamie as founder and first user; future practitioners, operational teams and organisations if later approved for external use.
- **Operational owner and decision authority:** Jamie Peppard during the founder-controlled phase.
- **Constraints:** Human approval of material meaning and consequence; no confidential employer, client or third-party information; proportionate legal, safety, security, ethical and human controls; no inferred publication or connection authority.

## Provisional value order

The following order is inferred from Jamie's direction and requires confirmation before it becomes authoritative:

1. The methodology must be understandable and produce useful operational outcomes.
2. AI may lead execution while humans retain control over value, judgement, authority and consequence.
3. The method must address operations as connected systems rather than isolated processes.
4. It must improve through evidence and feedback without silently changing approved meaning.
5. It should become scalable and commercially useful only after repeatable user value is demonstrated.
6. Delivery efficiency and technical elegance support these outcomes but do not outrank them.

Minimum acceptable outcome: a user receives evidence-based findings, options, recommendations and an implementation route; authoritative changes remain traceable and human-approved; confidential information is excluded.

## Findings

### Recorded facts

- Foundation v0.3 is the approved internal baseline.
- Jamie approved v0.4 for internal validation and authorised PR #6 for merge on 2026-07-20.
- The private application has no external AI connection and mainly applies deterministic rules to user input.
- The existing repository workflow can turn Jamie's direction into a checked proposal and explicit decision.
- No external feedback, analytics, notification or business-system connection is approved.

### Jamie's recorded judgement

- OPERATE is not the complete methodology.
- The methodology must cover operations as a whole and show how to become automation-ready, AI-ready and agentic-ready.
- The methodology and its update system should be developed in tandem.
- A delivery experience must return useful AI-generated analysis rather than leaving the user to perform the method.
- The present application should be shelved while these foundations are developed.

### AI inferences requiring validation

- The immediate constraint is methodology depth and output quality, not application engineering capacity.
- Jamie, Codex and GitHub are sufficient for the next internal MVP without another connection.
- Mobile notifications and multi-source feedback should follow proof that decision packets are useful.
- Detailed work-type modules should be prioritised from real cases rather than written comprehensively in advance.

### Evidence limitations

- This assessment relies primarily on one founder-user and repository evidence.
- No independent practitioner has used the v0.4 architecture.
- No operation has yet completed a full facilitated cycle under v0.4.
- Commercial need, willingness to pay and support cost are unknown.

### Confidence

- **High:** The project previously treated OPERATE too narrowly and the application did not return enough user value.
- **Medium:** The v0.4 architecture is a credible basis for internal validation.
- **Low:** The final module structure, product form and commercial model.

## Recommended target state

The next internal operating model should be:

1. Jamie provides direction, operational context and consequential decisions in ordinary language.
2. AI applies the methodology, returns a useful assessment and manages controlled repository execution.
3. Each case produces operational outputs and structured feedback about the method.
4. AI connects related signals and prepares a checked change proposal and decision packet.
5. Jamie approves, revises or rejects meaning and release.
6. Approved changes are versioned, distributed to authorised delivery surfaces and reviewed against outcomes.

No additional application or connection is required to test this model.
