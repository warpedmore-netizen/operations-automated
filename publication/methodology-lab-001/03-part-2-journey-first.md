# Part II – Start with the journey

## What this part establishes

An operation exists to produce an outcome for someone. Begin with the customer, service user or stakeholder journey rather than the internal organisation chart or the technology already available.

Follow what the person is trying to achieve:

- What triggered their need?
- What did they expect to happen?
- What did they have to provide, understand or repeat?
- Where did they wait, abandon, escalate or lose confidence?
- What outcome did they receive?
- What happened after the apparent end of the interaction?

The journey is not the whole operation. It is the route into it. Once a material point of friction, harm or unrealised value is visible, trace it back through the people, work, decisions, dependencies, information, controls and technology that created it.

## Why the order matters

Starting internally can optimise an activity that does not matter to the beneficiary, or make one team faster by pushing effort and failure elsewhere.

Starting with the journey gives the operation a reason to improve. Starting with the operation next gives the change enough depth to work.
