# Cross-functional interfaces

## Operations connects; it does not absorb

Operational outcomes cross functional boundaries. Operations Automated provides the connected view and makes the necessary interfaces and decisions explicit. It does not claim ownership of the full methods or authority of specialist disciplines.

Engage a specialist early enough to change the design, not merely to approve a finished proposal. Record advice, conditions, decisions and unresolved disagreement.

## Core interfaces

### Strategy and leadership

Connect the operation to intended outcomes, priorities, time horizon, business model and acceptable trade-offs. Escalate conflicts between local improvement and enterprise direction. Leadership retains strategy and commitment authority.

### Finance and commercial

Make lifecycle cost, affordability, value assumptions, benefits, uncertainty, cash or funding effects and opportunity cost visible. Finance retains accounting, tax, treasury, capital and financial approval authority.

### People and organisation

Identify role, capacity, skill, workload, consultation, fairness, wellbeing, reward and organisational effects. People specialists and authorised leaders retain formal employment, organisation and policy authority.

### Legal, risk and compliance

Identify rights, duties, contracts, regulations, risk ownership, control and assurance needs. Qualified specialists interpret obligations; authorised owners decide within their risk authority.

### Security, privacy and safety

Define information, access, threat, harm, safe-operation, monitoring and response conditions. The relevant specialists retain standards, acceptance and statutory authority.

### Data and knowledge

Agree definitions, ownership, quality, provenance, permitted use, retention, access and correction. Data and records authorities retain governance decisions.

### Technology, architecture and assets

Test feasibility, integration, lifecycle, resilience, support, technical debt and decommissioning. Architecture, engineering, service and asset authorities retain their decisions.

### Product, portfolio and change

Connect user value and operating outcomes to prioritisation, discovery, delivery, adoption and retirement. Portfolio and product authorities decide commitments; operational expertise must shape acceptance and sustainability.

### Suppliers and partners

Define service, evidence, data, control, escalation, continuity and exit. Procurement, commercial and legal authorities retain negotiation and contracting decisions.

### Customers, sales and marketing

Align the operational promise, demand shaping, eligibility, channels, communication and feedback. Market and customer authorities retain proposition and communication decisions, but should not promise unsupported operational outcomes.

### Audit and quality

Define evidence, conformance, independence, quality criteria and corrective action. Audit retains its independence; relevant authorities decide certification and formal quality status.

### Facilities, environment and sustainability

Identify physical workplace, equipment, location, accessibility, environmental and long-term effects. Qualified specialists and accountable leaders retain statutory and technical decisions.

## Interface contract

For any material interface, agree:

- shared outcome and boundary;
- what each party provides and receives;
- entry, acceptance and completion criteria;
- information, definitions and quality;
- timing, capacity and dependency;
- decision, approval and escalation rights;
- obligations, controls and evidence;
- exception, incident and recovery route;
- measures, review rhythm and improvement route; and
- owner on each side.

Do not use a responsibility matrix as a substitute for conversation and evidence. The contract should describe how work will actually cross the boundary.

## Resolve conflicts explicitly

Cross-functional tension is often legitimate: speed against control, local value against shared architecture, short-term affordability against lifecycle resilience, individual experience against policy consistency. State the competing outcomes and evidence. Generate options and conditions. Escalate to the authority able to resolve the trade-off rather than allowing it to reappear as delivery friction.

## Outputs and gate

Produce the material interface map, specialist involvement plan, operational interface contracts, retained advice and decisions, unresolved conflicts and escalation route.

The design is not ready when a material specialist is being asked to endorse a fixed solution, when authority is ambiguous, or when an external dependency has no acceptance, failure and exit conditions.
