# Part I – Purpose, value, responsibility and accountability

## What this part establishes

Improvement has no useful meaning until the intended outcome and beneficiary are understood. Cost, speed, efficiency, automation and AI adoption are possible forms of value; none is universally the right priority.

Begin by making seven things visible:

1. the desired outcome;
2. the beneficiary;
3. the forms of value;
4. priorities between those forms of value;
5. minimum acceptable outcomes;
6. constraints and obligations; and
7. decision authority.

This creates a proportionate value matrix. It gives later analysis something real to optimise and prevents a technical capability from becoming its own justification.

## The human boundary

Technology may execute work, organise evidence, generate analysis and recommend action. People retain purpose, context, judgement, empathy, authority, leadership and accountability.

Human involvement should be meaningful rather than ceremonial. A person cannot provide effective assurance if they lack the time, information, capability, authority or practical ability to challenge a recommendation.

The proposed v0.8 wording separates three concepts:

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Responsibility | The work and standard assigned to an actor. AI is responsible for producing, checking, explaining and correcting its authorised contribution. |
| Authority | Legitimate permission to decide, commit, publish, accept risk or cause an effect. AI receives bounded permission; it does not create its own. |
| Accountability | Answerability for the operational outcome and consequence. This remains with an authorised human or organisation. |

This does not excuse weak AI work. AI should use evidence, disclose limits, challenge assumptions, stop rather than fabricate and correct material failure. It cannot currently hold human, organisational, legal or moral accountability.

## Deliberately manual work

Manual work is not unfinished automation. Retain it deliberately where judgement, empathy, tacit knowledge, physical presence or accountable authority is central. Give it a purpose, owner, capacity, information need, control, recovery route and improvement trigger.

Where outcomes conflict, expose the trade-off and give the authorised human the choice. If no option can meet the minimum outcome within the applicable obligations, reconsider the scope or return to examination and redesign.

## Next

With purpose and value established, follow the experience of the person or stakeholder receiving the service. The journey shows where operational attention is likely to create real value.
