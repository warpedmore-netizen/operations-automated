# Part IV – Improve and implement with OPERATE

## What this part establishes

OPERATE is the improvement and implementation cycle inside Operations Automated:

1. **Observe** the real operation, evidence, demand and outcomes.
2. **Prioritise** using user-defined value, consequence, opportunity and constraints.
3. **Examine** causes, dependencies, assumptions and alternative explanations.
4. **Redesign** the operation, responsibilities, controls and experience.
5. **Automate** only the work for which technology is justified; the stage also permits a deliberate decision not to automate.
6. **Test** outcomes, failure, control, usability and recovery within a proportionate boundary.
7. **Evolve** by retaining lessons, decisions and improvements.

The cycle is not a compulsory form and does not require equal effort at every stage. Small reversible work may move quickly. Consequential change needs stronger evidence, named authority and assurance.

The user should receive useful outputs throughout: findings, options, recommendations, decisions, measures and working aids. Questions and records support the work; they are not the value by themselves.
