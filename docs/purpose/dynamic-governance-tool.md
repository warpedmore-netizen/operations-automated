# Dynamic Governance Tool — Product Purpose and Boundaries

## Control note

This proposed purpose separates the product currently described in retained artefacts as Connected Governance or the Operational Governance proof of concept. It does not approve its product meaning, name, migration, deployment or release.

## Why it exists

Help an organisation create, connect, maintain and evidence its own governance requirements, policies, controls, procedures, decisions, tests, findings and releases.

## Intended users

Initially the Operations Automated founder and authorised internal governance roles; later, if validated and separately approved, accountable governance, risk, control, policy and operational owners in customer organisations.

## Core outcome

An authorised user can turn governing sources and operational findings into traceable candidate requirements and linked governance, while keeping applicability, evidence, ownership, approval, publication and assurance explicit.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs may include authorised requirements, controlled documents, findings, decisions and evidence. Outputs may include draft requirements, policies, controls, procedures, tests, findings, relationships, release records and publication packages.

## Boundaries and non-goals

The product is not the Operations Automated Methodology, the private AI Workbench, legal or regulatory authority, a compliance certification, or an autonomous policy approver. It must not import the core project's internal database or approval authority.

## Information boundary

The product requires its own data classification, storage, identity, retention, deletion, security and recovery decisions. It may receive controlled findings or references from Operations Automated, not unbounded access to its operational memory.

## Authority boundary

AI may extract, connect, draft, challenge and test. An authorised human retains applicability judgements, company policy, control ownership, risk acceptance, release and publication.

## Relationship to Operations Automated

The Dynamic Governance Tool is a separate product and should move through a non-destructive migration to a separately controlled project and repository. Until then, retained implementation under `governance-poc/` and `governance-site/` is migration evidence, not shared product authority.

## Success evidence

Success requires a validated user problem, controlled data and authority model, recoverable storage, traceable source-to-governance relationships, useful operating evidence and a release lifecycle separate from Methodology and Workbench approval.
